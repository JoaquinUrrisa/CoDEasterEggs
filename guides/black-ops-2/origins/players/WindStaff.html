🟡 Wind Staff Player Guide (P4)

A pruned copy of the full guide, focused only on what the Wind Staff player needs.

Your zone: the middle of the map / No Man's Land — wherever the robots are walking. Your staff: Staff of Wind → Boreas' Fury. Your extra jobs: you are the gramophone carrier. Everyone's staff depends on you running the portal circuit, so treat that as your first priority once the map opens up.

Your parts come from inside the three giant robots, and only one foot per pass is lit at random. This is a patience job — start watching robot passes from round 1 and grab whatever comes up.

Job #1: the gramophone

The gramophone always spawns on the floor of the Excavation Site main chamber. Only one player can carry it, and it's the key to five different things — so it's yours.

The circuit

Go in map order so you're not backtracking:

  1. Fire tunnel — left of the spawn exit, by the Rituals box, on the way to Gen 3 → P1 takes the red gem
  2. Wind tunnel — near Generator 4, next to a lightning panel → you take the yellow gem
  3. Lightning tunnel — near Generator 5, opposite the soul chest → P3 takes the purple gem
  4. Ice tunnel — across from Generator 6, left as you exit the church → P2 takes the blue gem

How it works: place the gramophone on the table at the end of the tunnel while holding that element's record. The gateway builds itself, then run straight into it. The portal falls apart after ~15 seconds.

Key rules:

  • A gem can only be taken if you entered through that element's own tunnel. So all four trips are needed.
  • Once a player has used a portal once, they can rebuild that portal from inside the Crazy Place without the gramophone — so later Crazy Place trips (riddles, charging, Step 7) don't need you.
  • Announce each trip. Everyone should come along; it's four free portal unlocks per person.

Build the Staff of Wind

Built at the yellow pedestal, lowest level of the Excavation Site.

Parts — one inside each giant robot

The three robots are Freya (left, church side), Odin (centre, Excavation Site) and Thor (right, over Gens 2 & 3). Each head holds one part.

How to get inside:

  1. Watch a robot approach. One foot per pass has glowing lights under it — and which foot is randomised.
  2. Shoot the glowing foot.
  3. Stand under it and let it step on you → you're teleported into that robot's head.
  4. Grab the part, then eject (or ride out the purge).

A robot foot that you haven't shot instantly downs you. 💡 The Golden Helmet (dug by a golden shovel — ask the ice player) makes robot feet harmless. This is the single best quality-of-life item for your role, and it also helps in Steps 2 and 3. 💡 While inside Freya, there's a red radio opposite the audio log — one of the three for the Shepherd of Fire music easter egg. Free, takes a second.

Wind tunnel → yellow gem

Near Generator 4, next to one of the lightning-upgrade panels. Gramophone + yellow record → portal → run through. The yellow pedestal opens and the wind gem rises out.

Then build at the yellow pedestal on the lowest Excavation level.

Upgrade to Boreas' Fury

A — Crazy Place riddle (the concentric rings)

Above the wind portal are four concentric rings, each carrying four symbols. Each pillar shows a target symbol. Shoot a ring with the Wind Staff to rotate it. Align the rings so that each row of symbols adds up to the value on the pillar it points at.

Reading the symbols (base 4): count the lines in each shape.

  • One shape → its line count
  • Two shapes → (first × 4) + last
  • Three shapes → (first × 16) + (second × 4) + last

The correct final arrangement never changes— only the starting rotation is randomised. You can just match the key image instead of doing the maths. 💡 Best vantage point: stand between the rocks to the right of the portal gateway.

When it's right, the inner rings rotate up with an audio cue.

B — The three smoking stone balls

Three stone balls emit smoke. Shoot each with the Wind Staff to rotate it until its smoke blows toward the Excavation Site.

  1. Behind the Stamin-Up machine (Generator 5)
  2. Between Generator 4 and the Wind tunnel
  3. On the outside section of No Man's Land, on the tank's return path
Ball #3 must be approached from the right sideso you can push the smoke the correct way. Either backtrack the tank path from the church, or jump off the tank as it returns from the Tank Station. Coordinate with the Lightning player's tank run.

Audio cue + beam of light from the Excavation Site when all three are aimed correctly.

C — Ring puzzle (Excavation Site, lower level)

Four floating rings, four levers (two on the walkway, two on the wooden scaffolding). Turn them until all four ring lights are yellow, then shoot the yellow orb with the Wind Staff.

One element at a time — queue with your teammates.

D — Charge in the Crazy Place

Place the staff on the yellow pedestal in the Crazy Place, kill ~20 zombies inside, pick up Boreas' Fury.

Do this during the Fire player's grate farm — their riddle doesn't consume souls, so your staff charges off their kills.

Your weapon

Fires a wind blast that flings zombies away and kills them on impact. Upgraded (Boreas' Fury) it holds to charge into a vortex that sucks in and shreds a group — one of the best panic buttons in the game. Melee one-hit-kills until round 16. Press left on the D-pad for Sekhmet's Vigor to revive a downed teammate at range.

If you lose the staff it returns to its Excavation Site pedestal with the upgrade intact.

Shared jobs (everyone does these)

G-Strike beacon — do it early

  1. Tank Station (Gen 2) — take a dirty stone tablet (no prompt, just press action). Four exist, one per player.
  2. Carry it to the church basin of holy water, insert it.
  3. 20 melee kills near the basin → take the cleansed tablet.
  4. Return it to the Tank Station table without stepping in mud (use the wooden beams). Muddy = re-cleanse at the basin.
  5. 20 more melee kills near the table → G-Strike Beacon.

Soul chests → One Inch Punch

Four chests, 30 kills each, in giant footprints: Gen 5 / Stamin-Up · Excavation→church path (safe, not in Odin's lane) · No Man's Land entrance→Gen 4 · map corner by Gen 4 / MP-40. Claim at a Rituals of the Ancients board (Gen 1 or Gen 6) — aim at the bottom-most medal.

You're the robot player — you'll see this happen.If a robot steps on an unfinished chest it closes and resets. Call out robot lanes so the squad farms the safe chest first.

Easter egg steps — your part

  • Step 2: Boreas' Fury goes into Odin — the centre robot, the one that treads the Excavation Site. You're already the robot expert, so you'll likely be placing more than one staff. Remember: Ice → Freya (left/church), Wind → Odin (centre), Lightning → Thor (right/Gens 2&3), Fire → staff room pedestal.
  • Step 3: you're the natural button presser — enter a robot, press the red button, while a teammate throws the G-Strike onto the seal behind/right of Generator 5. Count them down before you press. After Step 2, all three robots roam at once and only one foot is lit at a time, so expect a few attempts.
  • Step 6: you need your own Iron Fists — ~20 One Inch Punch hits on white-glowing Crusaders inside the Excavation Site. Don't punch ones still climbing through walls.
  • Step 7: Boreas' Fury on its Crazy Place pedestal, help kill 100 Templars inside.

Reminders

  • Black record first — the whole squad is blocked until the lower Excavation levels are open.
  • Don't end a round while it's snowing (ice player's parts).
  • Don't grab Nukes.
  • Keep the Mauser C96 and Pack-a-Punch it; buy the MP40 early (there's a wall-buy near Gen 4, on your patch).
  • Ask the ice player for a Golden Helmet as soon as they have a golden shovel.
