# 🔵 Ice Staff Player Guide (P2)

A pruned copy of the [full guide](../index.html), focused only on what the **Ice Staff** player needs.

**Your zone:** Spawn → Workshop → middle of the map, then the church.
**Your staff:** Staff of Ice → **Ull's Arrow**.
**Your extra jobs:** **shovel duty and digging**, and later you become the squad's **Zombie Blood
generator** — which is what unlocks Step 5.

> ⚠️ **You are the most time-critical player in the squad.** All three of your parts come from dig
> spots, and dig spots only yield ice parts **while it is snowing**. Snow happens roughly on
> **rounds 2–4**, again around **round 10**, then randomly. Miss the first window and you stall the
> whole run.

---

## Your checklist

- [ ] **Grab a shovel in round 1** (spawn, by the starting radio)
- [ ] Dig everything in the **starting area** while it snows (rounds 2–4)
- [ ] Dig the **middle of the map** while it snows
- [ ] Dig **near the church** while it snows (may need the round-10 window)
- [ ] Grab the **blue record** at the Tank Station (Gen 2)
- [ ] Enter the Crazy Place via the **Ice tunnel** (church / Gen 6) → take the blue gem
- [ ] Build the staff at the blue pedestal
- [ ] Upgrade A — six floating panels puzzle in the Crazy Place
- [ ] Upgrade B — freeze + shatter the three water gravestones
- [ ] Upgrade C — rings all blue, shoot the blue orb
- [ ] Upgrade D — charge on the blue pedestal (~20 kills)
- [ ] Optional but strong: **dig 30 spots → golden shovel → Golden Helmet**
- [ ] Shared: G-Strike, soul chests, One Inch Punch

## Round plan

| Rounds | You do |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Take a shovel immediately.** Kill for points. Help cap **Gen 1**. |

## Build the Staff of Ice

Built at the **blue pedestal**, lowest level of the Excavation Site (opened with the gramophone + black record).

### Parts — dig them, and only while it snows

Three parts, one in each zone:

1. **Starting area** — spawn through to the workshop, either side.
2. **Middle of the map**.
3. **Near the church**.

![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/5/5c/Staff_of_Ice_Part_Origins_BOII.png)

**How to work a snow window:**
- The moment snow starts, call it out on comms: *"snowing — nobody end the round."*
- Sweep your zone and dig **every** pile. Digs are random; the part is in there somewhere.
- Bad digs happen: a zombie, one live grenade, or two live grenades. Back off after you press dig.
- Good digs: Zombie Blood, Blood Money, Mauser C96, Remington 870, Ballista, M1216, DSR 50. Rare: Max Ammo, Double Points, Fire Sale, Insta-Kill, Nuke.

> 💡 Ask a teammate to bring a second shovel and dig alongside you during snow. Four shovels exist —
> two in spawn (by the starting radio, and in the corridor by the Gen 3 door), and two of these three:
> church by the tank treads · Wind tunnel by Gen 4 · Lightning tunnel by Gen 5.

### Blue record

**Tank Station near Generator 2**, on one of the shelves.

### Ice tunnel → blue gem

The **Ice tunnel** is **across from Generator 6, on the left as you exit the church**. The gramophone
carrier places the gramophone at the end of the tunnel with the **blue record** → portal → run through.

Inside, the **blue pedestal** opens and the ice gem rises out. Take it.
**Only obtainable if you entered through the Ice tunnel.**

Then build at the **blue pedestal** on the lowest Excavation level.

> 💡 The Maxis Drone **Frame** can spawn right here — in the Ice tunnel, in front of the portal. Grab it.

## Upgrade to Ull's Arrow

### A — Crazy Place riddle (the panel-matching one)

In the ice section of the Crazy Place:
- **Above the portal**: **six floating stone panels**, each showing a base-4 symbol.
- **On the wall**: a **blue ice panel** showing a pattern of **dots** (base-3).

Read the dot pattern currently on the wall, find the floating panel with the **matching value**, and
**shoot that panel with the Ice Staff**. It flips over and the wall shows the next pattern.
Repeat until **all six panels are flipped**.

![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/3/39/Ice_Staff_Challenge_Symbols_Key_Origins_BOII.png)

> ⚠️ **Shoot the wrong panel and every panel you already flipped resets.** Take your time.
> There are twelve possible dot patterns; only six appear per game, and the pairing is randomised —
> so you can't memorise a fixed answer. Use the key image above.
> 💡 Have a teammate hold the horde while you read. Getting hit mid-puzzle is how people misfire.

### B — The three water gravestones

Three gravestones with **water flowing out of them**. For each: **freeze it with the Ice Staff, then
destroy it with a bullet weapon.**

1. **Right of the MP40 wall-buy near Generator 4**
   ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/8/8b/Ice_Staff_Challenge_Water_Tombstone_1_Origins_BOII.png)
2. **Between the Excavation Site and a giant footprint**, close to Generator 4
   ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/1/17/Ice_Staff_Challenge_Water_Tombstone_2_Origins_BOII.png)
3. **On top of the small hill** to the right of the back exit of the Generator 2 bunker, next to the downed mech's hand
   ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/1/18/Ice_Staff_Challenge_Water_Tombstone_3_Origins_BOII.png)

When all three are gone you get an audio cue and a beam of light from the Excavation Site.

### C — Ring puzzle (Excavation Site, lower level)

Four floating rings, four levers (two on the walkway, two on the wooden scaffolding). Turn them until
**all four ring lights are blue**, then **shoot the blue orb with the Ice Staff**.

> ⚠️ One element at a time — queue with your teammates.

### D — Charge in the Crazy Place

Place the staff on the **blue pedestal** in the Crazy Place, kill **~20 zombies** inside, pick up
**Ull's Arrow**.

> 💡 Best moment to do this: while the Fire player is farming their 30 grate kills. Their riddle
> doesn't eat souls, so yours charges for free off their kills.

## 🩸 Your superpower: the Zombie Blood farm

Shoot **three flaming carts** with the **Ice Staff** (works upgraded or not) → a **Zombie Blood**
power-up spawns next to the scaffolding by **Pack-a-Punch**.

1. Just outside the Excavation Site, by the **MP-40 wall-buy**
2. By the footprint with the stone chest outside **Generator 5 / Stamin-Up**
3. Just outside the trenches behind the Excavation Site (church side), more toward **Generator 4**

**Rules:**
- **Once per round.**
- **Does not work while it's raining** (the carts aren't lit).
- If you shoot all three but nobody picks the power-up up, they won't relight that round.

**Why it matters:**
- **Step 5 (Skewer the winged beast)** is entirely gated on Zombie Blood. You supply it.
- With a **golden shovel** + Zombie Blood you can see glowing orange dig spots that give an
  **Empty Perk Bottle** → a **5th perk** for whoever digs it.
- Zombie Blood makes you invisible to zombies, Panzers and hounds — great for reviving and for
  capping a generator under pressure.

## Your weapon

Blasts of ice that slow, then freeze and shatter zombies. In **BO2 the initial blast doesn't kill
instantly** — the zombie can still swing at you before it shatters, so keep spacing (BO3 kills instantly).

Upgraded (**Ull's Arrow**): wider area, longer range, and **hold fire to charge** — releases a blizzard
that pulls nearby zombies in and freezes/kills them instantly. Melee one-hit-kills until round 16.
Press left on the D-pad for **Sekhmet's Vigor** to revive a downed teammate at range.

If you lose the staff it returns to its Excavation Site pedestal **with the upgrade intact**.

## Golden shovel (worth it, and it's your lane)

**Dig 30 spots in one game** → your shovel goes gold. It unlocks better digs (KSG, AK74u, STG-44,
MP-40, Claymores, **Golden Helmet**) and cuts the chance of bad results.

- **Golden Helmet** = giant robot feet no longer down you. Hand it to the Wind player if they don't
  have one — it makes their part hunt and Steps 2/3 far safer.
- Golden shovel + Zombie Blood = **Empty Perk Bottles**.

You'll be digging constantly anyway during snow windows, so 30 comes naturally.

## Shared jobs (everyone does these)

### G-Strike beacon — do it early

1. **Tank Station (Gen 2)** — take a **dirty stone tablet** (no prompt, just press action). Four exist, one per player.
2. Carry it to the **church basin of holy water**, insert it.
3. **20 melee kills** near the basin → take the cleansed tablet.
4. Return it to the **Tank Station table without stepping in mud** (use the wooden beams). Muddy = re-cleanse at the basin.
5. **20 more melee kills** near the table → **G-Strike Beacon**.

### Soul chests → One Inch Punch

Four chests, 30 kills each, in giant footprints:
Gen 5 / Stamin-Up · Excavation→church path (safe, not in Odin's lane) · No Man's Land entrance→Gen 4 · map corner by Gen 4 / MP-40.
Claim at a **Rituals of the Ancients** board (Gen 1 or Gen 6) — aim at the **bottom-most medal**.

> ⚠️ Robot steps reset an unfinished chest. Dig-spot zombies don't count.

### Easter egg steps — your part

- **Step 2:** **Ull's Arrow goes into Freya** — the **left** robot, the one that treads around the Church.
- **Step 5:** you run the cart farm and hand out Zombie Bloods. Stack a couple before the squad tries the plane/pilot.
- **Step 6:** you need your own **Iron Fists** — ~20 One Inch Punch hits on white-glowing Crusaders inside the Excavation Site. Don't punch ones still climbing through walls.
- **Step 7:** Ull's Arrow on its Crazy Place pedestal, help kill 100 Templars inside.

## Reminders

- **Call out snow every time.** Nobody ends the round until you say so.
- Don't grab **Nukes**.
- Keep the **Mauser C96** and Pack-a-Punch it; buy the **MP40** early (Tank Station wall-buy is on your route).
- You need a **bullet weapon** for the gravestone step — don't run staff-only.
