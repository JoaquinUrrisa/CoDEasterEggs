🟣 Lightning Staff Player Guide (P3)

A pruned copy of the full guide, focused only on what the Lightning Staff player needs.

Your zone: the tank route — church ↔ Tank Station (Gen 2). Your staff: Staff of Lightning → Kimat's Bite. Your extra jobs: tank driver, and turning the seven electrical panels scattered across the whole map (you can pre-turn these from round 1 — do it).

Your parts need the tank, and the tank starts at the church — so you can't begin until the church path is open. Meanwhile, pre-turn every electrical panel you walk past.

Build the Staff of Lightning

Built at the purple pedestal, lowest level of the Excavation Site (opened with the gramophone + black record).

The tank

  • Parked under the church near Generator 6. Climb on and use the control panel — 500 points.
  • It drives the muddy road to the Tank Station (Gen 2), then needs ~2 minutes to cool down. Fewer players riding = faster cooldown.
  • Once it's been activated at least once from the control panel, you can call it for 500 from the switch beside where it stops, at either end.
  • Zombies can climb on while it moves; the side flamethrowers help but don't rely on them.

Parts — all three are jump-offs from the moving tank

Bring a second player.With two of you on the return run you can split the two jump points and get both parts in one trip. Alone it's very hard to catch both. 💡 After all three parts are collected, a random power-up spawns at each jump-off point once per round. Free Max Ammos — keep riding.

Purple record

Near Generator 4, one of:

  • Inside the tunnel by the Crazy Place gateway
  • On the bandwagon to the left as you enter the tunnel
  • On the table next to the Der Wunderfizz machine

Lightning tunnel → purple gem

The Lightning tunnel is near Generator 5, on the opposite side from the soul chest. The gramophone carrier places the gramophone at the end of the tunnel with the purple record → portal → run through.

Inside, the purple pedestal opens and the lightning gem rises out. Take it. Only obtainable if you entered through the Lightning tunnel.

Then build at the purple pedestal on the lowest Excavation level.

Upgrade to Kimat's Bite

A — Crazy Place riddle (the keyboard) — memorise this

Near the portal in the lightning section, purple triangles on the wall form a keyboard. Opposite walls show three chords of three notes each. Play them with the Lightning Staff.

The chords never change. Number the seven bottom-row keys 1–7, left to right. Ignore the top row entirely. Play:

136  →  357  →  246

B — The seven electrical panels

Eight panels exist around the map; one is pre-completed, leaving seven. Turn each knob to the correct direction:

#LocationPoint it
1Right next to Generator 5Down
2Church basement, next to the torch numbered 3Right
3Church upstairs, next to the repairable windowUp
4Next to the Wind tunnel, right of its entranceUp
5Spawn room, at the bottom of the stairsLeft
6Tank Station (Gen 2), left of the back doorDown
7Behind the Excavation Site, next to the path toward the churchUp

How it behaves: after the Crazy Place riddle is solved, the panels spark one at a time, in the order listed above — panel 2 won't spark until panel 1 is correct.

Huge time saver: pre-turn every panel from round 1.You can turn them at any point in the game, riddle or no riddle, as long as you remember how far each knob is turned. If they're already in the right positions, this whole step resolves the moment you finish the keyboard. ⚠️ There's a decoy 8th panel just before the No Man's Land door in the Workshop, on the right. It does nothing — ignore it.

When all seven are correct you get an audio cue and a beam of light from the Excavation Site.

C — Ring puzzle (Excavation Site, lower level)

Four floating rings, four levers (two on the walkway, two on the wooden scaffolding). Turn them until all four ring lights are purple, then shoot the purple orb with the Lightning Staff.

One element at a time — queue with your teammates.

D — Charge in the Crazy Place

Place the staff on the purple pedestal in the Crazy Place and kill 25 zombies inside (lightning needs slightly more than the other three). Pick up Kimat's Bite.

Do this during the Fire player's grate farm — their riddle doesn't consume souls, so your staff charges off their kills.

Your weapon

Chains lightning between 2–3 zombies per bolt (shoot at their feet to catch more). Upgraded (Kimat's Bite) it chains further and holds to charge — six ammo per charged shot, three shots per mag — firing a slow orb that arcs lightning into everything it passes.

Honest expectation: normal shots only make crawlers past ~round 25 unless you headshot; the charged shot stays useful to round 30–40. It's the weakest of the four late, so plan on a Pack-a-Punched gun or the Magna Collider alongside it.

Melee one-hit-kills until round 16. Press left on the D-pad for Sekhmet's Vigor to revive a downed teammate at range.

BO2 quirk: the staff comes off the pedestal with 9 shots instead of 12. Not a bug on your end.

If you lose the staff it returns to its Excavation Site pedestal with the upgrade intact.

Shared jobs (everyone does these)

G-Strike beacon — do it early

You're the tank driver, so the church ↔ Tank Station shuttle is literally your route.

  1. Tank Station (Gen 2) — take a dirty stone tablet (no prompt, just press action). Four exist, one per player.
  2. Carry it to the church basin of holy water, insert it.
  3. 20 melee kills near the basin → take the cleansed tablet.
  4. Return it to the Tank Station table without stepping in mud. ⚠️ Riding the tank is the clean way to cross — the road is the mud. Otherwise stick to the wooden beams. Muddy = re-cleanse at the basin.
  5. 20 more melee kills near the table → G-Strike Beacon.

Soul chests → One Inch Punch

Four chests, 30 kills each, in giant footprints: Gen 5 / Stamin-Up · Excavation→church path (safe, not in Odin's lane) · No Man's Land entrance→Gen 4 · map corner by Gen 4 / MP-40. Claim at a Rituals of the Ancients board (Gen 1 or Gen 6) — aim at the bottom-most medal.

Robot steps reset an unfinished chest. Dig-spot zombies don't count.

Maxis Drone parts on your route

  • Frame can spawn near the crossbone signs on the tank's muddy paths to Gen 4 and Gen 5 — you drive past both. Grab it.

Easter egg steps — your part

  • Step 2: Kimat's Bite goes into Thor — the right robot, the one that treads over Generators 2 & 3.
  • Step 3: you're a good candidate for the G-Strike thrower (position at Gen 5 and watch the seal) or the button-presser inside the robot. Decide before someone enters.
  • Step 6: you need your own Iron Fists — ~20 One Inch Punch hits on white-glowing Crusaders inside the Excavation Site. Don't punch ones still climbing through walls.
  • Step 7: Kimat's Bite on its Crazy Place pedestal, help kill 100 Templars inside.

Reminders

  • Turn panels from round 1. It's free progress and it saves a full lap later.
  • Don't end a round while it's snowing (ice player's parts).
  • Don't grab Nukes.
  • Keep the Mauser C96 and Pack-a-Punch it; buy the MP40 (Tank Station wall-buy is on your route).
  • Budget 500 for the tank, and remember the cooldown — don't strand yourself at the wrong end.
