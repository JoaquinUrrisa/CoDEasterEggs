# Origins Easter Egg — "Little Lost Girl" (4-Player Squad Guide)

> **This is the long-form reference.** The interactive version — role picker, checklists, diagram/photo switch — is [`index.html`](./index.html) in this folder, published at
> <https://joaquinurrisa.github.io/CoDEasterEggs/guides/black-ops-2/origins/>.
> Both are kept; this one is easier to search, skim and copy from.

Step-by-step guide to completing the **Little Lost Girl** easter egg on **Origins**, with a squad of **4**.
Written for **Black Ops 2 on PS5** (PS Plus / backwards-compatible release). Everything here also
applies to the **Black Ops 3 / Zombies Chronicles** remaster — see [BO2 vs BO3 differences](#bo2-vs-bo3-differences)
for the handful of things that change.

> **Note on the original draft:** the skeleton said "Black Ops 3". The steps below are written from
> BO2-specific sources (locations, counts and puzzle layouts are identical in both versions).

The easter egg is a series of 8 named steps. Some are strictly ordered, many prerequisites can be
done in parallel. **The strategy in this document is built around parallelising work across 4 players
and finishing in as few rounds as possible** — rounds get exponentially harder, so early-round
progress is the single biggest factor in whether you finish.

Each player owns one staff and follows their own sub-guide:

| Player | Role | Sub-guide |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Fire Staff** + church/Gen 6 side, plane, Panzer | [FireStaff.md](players/FireStaff.md) |
| P2 | **Ice Staff** + shovel/digging, Zombie Blood engine | [IceStaff.md](players/IceStaff.md) |
| P3 | **Lightning Staff** + tank driver, electrical panels | [LightningStaff.md](players/LightningStaff.md) |
| P4 | **Wind Staff** + robots, gramophone carrier | [WindStaff.md](players/WindStaff.md) |

---

## Steps overview

1. **Secure the keys** — build *and* upgrade all four elemental staffs
2. **Ascend from darkness** — place the four ultimate staffs in the robot/staff-room pedestals
3. **Rain fire** — robot button + G-Strike on the seal near Generator 5
4. **Unleash the horde** — Maxis Drone into the pit, kill 10 Panzersoldats
5. **Skewer the winged beast** — Zombie Blood, shoot down the glowing plane, kill the pilot zombie
6. **Wield a fist of iron** — every player upgrades the One Inch Punch to Iron Fists
7. **Raise hell** — 4 staffs in the Crazy Place pedestals, 100 Templar kills inside (**trophy pops here**)
8. **Freedom** — Maxis Drone into the portal, then touch the blue rock (**ends the game**)

**Prerequisites you must build along the way** (all covered below):
Gramophone + 5 records · 4 Elemental Staffs (+ upgrades) · G-Strike beacon · One Inch Punch ·
Maxis Drone · all 6 generators on.

### Generators (200 points **per player** = 800 in a 4-man)

1. Spawn room, upper level
2. Tank Station, between Workshop and Spawn
3. Next to Speed Cola (alternate spawn↔workshop path)
4. No Man's Land, left on entry — Juggernog + Wind tunnel
5. No Man's Land, right on entry — Stamin-Up + Lightning tunnel
6. Behind the church, furthest from spawn

Everyone standing on the platform fills the bar faster. Whoever paid gets their points back on
capture (if they're on the pad and not down); everyone else on the pad gets +100.
**All 6 active = Pack-a-Punch opens at the top of the Excavation Site.**

> ⚠️ **Generator attacks:** from round 10, with 4+ generators powered, Templar zombies periodically
> attack a random active generator. If it goes down, Pack-a-Punch dies and you must re-cap it.
> Killing the attackers drops a **Max Ammo**. A Monkey Bomb or G-Strike thrown in the area stops them.
> **Steps 1, 2 and 8 will not complete unless all 6 generators are on**, so defend them.

## Team plan (who does what, and when)

### Role assignment

| Player | Staff | Primary zone | Secondary duties |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Fire | Church / Gen 6 | Shoot down the orange plane, kill the round-8 Panzer, red record |
| **P2** | Ice | Spawn → Workshop → middle, church | **Shovel duty**, digs during snow, later the Zombie Blood farm |
| **P3** | Lightning | Tank route (church ↔ Tank Station) | 7 electrical panels, blue record en route, tank calls |
| **P4** | Wind | Middle / No Man's Land | **Carries the gramophone**, robot entries, black + yellow records |

### Round-by-round target schedule

Treat these as targets, not hard rules. Communicate before ending any round.

**Rounds 1–2 — setup**
- Kill with the Mauser C96 for points; **headshots count toward the 115-headshot Ritual**.
- P2 grabs a shovel immediately (spawn, by the starting radio). P4 grabs the second (corridor by the Gen 3 door). Two more shovels exist (church by the tank treads, Wind tunnel by Gen 4, Lightning tunnel by Gen 5) — get all four if convenient.
- Cap **Generator 1**. Take the free **Zombie Blood** from the Rituals of the Ancients box at Gen 1 (4-player only; solo gets Double Points).
- P4 picks up the **gramophone** (always on the floor in the Excavation Site main chamber) and hunts the **black record** (outside the Excavation Site: by the excavation sign, in the wheelbarrow next to Pack-a-Punch, or in the trench by the church debris).

**Rounds 2–4 — the first snow window (CRITICAL)**
- It snows roughly on **rounds 2–4**, again around **round 10**, then randomly.
- **P2 digs every dig spot** in the spawn/workshop area and the middle of the map while it is snowing — this is the only way to get Ice Staff parts.
- **Do not end a round while it is snowing** unless P2 says so.
- Everyone else: open the doors toward Gen 2 and Gen 3, cap both.
- P4: place the gramophone + black record in the Excavation Site to **open the lower levels** (staff pedestals + ring puzzle).

**Rounds 4–7 — open the map**
- Open the **No Man's Land** door before round 8 (Panzer must spawn on round 8 for the Fire Staff part).
- Cap **Gen 4** and **Gen 5**.
- Open the path to the **church**, cap **Gen 6**.
- P1: Gen 6 Rituals box pops open with a **Fire Staff part**; grab the **red record**.
- P1: the **orange glowing plane** spawns the round *after* the church path is opened — shoot it down, part drops by the soul chest closest to the Excavation Site.
- P3: activate the tank at the church (500), ride it, collect Lightning parts.
- P4: work robot passes for the three Wind parts.
- Everyone: start the **soul chests** (4 chests, 30 kills each) whenever you fight near one.

**Round 8 — Panzer**
- P1 kills the first Panzersoldat → drops the last **Fire Staff part**. This is the only way to get it.

**Rounds 8–12 — gramophone circuit + build all staffs**
- P4 carries the gramophone around this loop, opening one portal per element. Everyone follows in, the staff owner takes their gem:
  1. **Fire tunnel** (spawn↔Gen 3) — red record → fire gem
  2. **Wind tunnel** (Gen 4) — yellow record → wind gem
  3. **Lightning tunnel** (Gen 5) — purple record → lightning gem
  4. **Ice tunnel** (church / Gen 6) — blue record → ice gem
- Each owner builds their staff at the matching coloured pedestal on the **lowest level of the Excavation Site**.
- Round-10 snow: P2 finishes any missing Ice parts.
- In parallel, start **G-Strike tablets** (all four players can run their own tablet at the same time).

**Rounds 12+ — upgrades and the easter egg**
- Everyone does their Crazy Place riddle + surface task, then queues for the **ring puzzle** (one staff at a time).
- Charge all four staffs in the Crazy Place — **you can charge multiple staffs at once**.
- Build the **Maxis Drone**.
- Finish the **soul chests** → One Inch Punch for everybody.
- Then run steps 2 → 8 back-to-back.

### What can run in parallel

| Can be done simultaneously | Must be sequential |
|---|---|
| All 4 staff part hunts | Gramophone portals (one gramophone) |
| All 4 G-Strike tablets | The ring puzzle (one colour at a time) |
| All 4 Crazy Place riddles | Staff upgrade order: riddle → surface task → rings → charge |
| Soul chests (any player's kills count) | Steps 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 |
| Charging multiple staffs in the Crazy Place | Step 1 & 2 need all 6 generators on |

## Steps

### Step 1: Secure the keys

**Goal:** build and upgrade all four staffs to their ultimate forms.

As soon as the round starts, each player works on their own staff (see the per-player guides).
While progressing, cap generators. When a player finishes their staff, they roll onto the shared
objectives: G-Strike, soul chests, Maxis Drone.

**Requirements:**

1. **Gramophone + records.** The gramophone always spawns on the floor of the Excavation Site main
   chamber. You need 5 records:
   - **Black** — opens the lower levels of the Excavation Site (staff pedestals + rings). Found outside the Excavation Site: next to the excavation sign, in the wheelbarrow by Pack-a-Punch, or in the trench near the church debris.
   - **Red** (fire), **Blue** (ice), **Yellow** (wind), **Purple** (lightning) — each opens its elemental portal to the Crazy Place. Locations in the staff sections below.
   - Only one player carries the gramophone at a time. Once a portal has been used, **any player who has used it can rebuild that portal from inside the Crazy Place** without the gramophone.

2. **Four staffs.** Each staff = **3 parts + 1 elemental gem** (gem from the Crazy Place, only obtainable through that element's own tunnel), built at its coloured pedestal on the lowest level of the Excavation Site.

3. **Four upgrades.** Each staff needs the same four sub-steps — see [Staff upgrade pattern](#staff-upgrade-pattern-all-four-staffs).

> ⚠️ **Step 1 only registers once all 6 generators have been on at the same time at least once
> *after* every staff was built.** If the step doesn't tick, re-cap everything.

Also build in parallel (details in [Other stuff](#other-stuff)):

- **G-Strike beacon** — required for Step 3. At least one player needs it; two is safer.
- **One Inch Punch** — required by *every* player for Step 6.
- **Maxis Drone** — required for Steps 4 and 8.

### Step 2: Ascend from darkness

Once all four staffs are **upgraded** and all six generators have been on together at least once,
four new pedestals appear. Each one only accepts a specific staff:

| Staff | Pedestal |
|---|---|
| **Ull's Arrow** (Ice, blue) | **Freya** — the left robot, treads around the Church |
| **Boreas' Fury** (Wind, yellow) | **Odin** — the centre robot, treads the Excavation Site |
| **Kimat's Bite** (Lightning, purple) | **Thor** — the right robot, treads over Generators 2 & 3 |
| **Kagutsuchi's Blood** (Fire, red) | **Staff room** — the fourth pedestal in front of the original four, lowest level of the Excavation Site |

To enter a robot: **shoot the glowing foot**, then let that robot step on you — you're teleported into
its head. Only one foot is lit per pass and it's randomised, so be patient.

Order doesn't matter. Samantha speaks and the staffs return to the staff room when it's done.
Staffs cannot be picked back up until the step completes.

> ⚠️ **All six generators must be active** or the pedestals won't despawn and the step won't register.

![Fire staff in its pedestal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/6/64/Fire_Staff_pedestal_Origins_BOII.png)

**BO2-only shortcut (glitch):** put the Fire Staff into the newly spawned staff-room pedestal, then
press action on its *original* pedestal to get the staff back. Repeat four times. The game counts all
four placements and the step completes. Mostly cosmetic side effects. Use at your own discretion.

### Step 3: Rain fire

**Prerequisite:** at least one player has the **G-Strike beacon**. Get it early — it needs 40 melee
kills and that gets ugly in high rounds.

After Step 2, all three robots roam continuously and only one foot is lit at a time.

1. **Player A** enters a robot (shoot the lit foot, get stepped on) and presses the **red button** that has appeared inside the head.
2. **Player B** — already standing near Generator 5 — immediately throws a **G-Strike onto the seal**: a big circle of cracked stone on the ground **behind and to the right of Generator 5**, out of bounds.
3. The robots fire artillery on the seal and crack it open. Samantha talks; short audio cue.

![The red button inside the robot](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/0/0b/G-Strike_button_Origins_BOII.png)

**Timing is tight.** If the beacon lands off-target it vanishes and the Demonic Announcer laughs —
retry. Have Player B pre-primed and looking at the seal before Player A presses the button.

*Solo/duo variant:* enter **Odin** from a footprint (the Stamin-Up side is easier to aim from),
press the red button as Maxis says **"one"** during the purge countdown (being purged is faster than
ejecting), then sprint to the seal priming the G-Strike on the way.

---

### Step 4: Unleash the horde

1. **Build the Maxis Drone** (parts below) at a workbench.
2. Deploy the drone **as close as possible to the broken seal**. He flies into the pit.
3. **10 Panzersoldats** fly out (4-player count; solo gets 2) and attack.
4. Kill them all.

![Panzers flying out of the pit](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/7/70/Panzer_Soldats_flying_out_of_pit_Origins_BOII.png)

**Loadout advice:** upgraded staffs, Ray Gun Mark II, G-Strikes, Pack-a-Punched LMGs. Split them up —
don't let two grab the same player. You can free a grabbed teammate by shooting the small red light
on the Panzer's claw arm.

The drone will not respawn at its station until you get it back in Step 5.

---

### Step 5: Skewer the winged beast

1. Get a **Zombie Blood** power-up. Fastest repeatable source: shoot the **three flaming carts** with the **Ice Staff** (upgraded or not) — once per round, and it does not work while it's raining. Zombie Blood then spawns by the scaffolding near Pack-a-Punch.
2. While Zombie Blood is active, a **glowing plane** is visible in the sky above the Excavation Site. **Shoot it down.**
3. Its **zombie pilot** drops to the ground and runs **clockwise** around the Excavation Site. He is *only* visible in Zombie Blood, sounds like a Templar but looks like a normal zombie. Run **counter-clockwise** to meet him.
4. Kill him → he drops the **upgraded Maxis Drone** (Pack-a-Punched MG08/15). Pick it up.

![Upgraded Maxis Drone drop](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/8/8b/Maxis_Drone_upgraded_dropped_Origins_BOII.png)

**Tip:** stack Zombie Bloods before starting — you may need more than one attempt, and the timer is short.

---

### Step 6: Wield a fist of iron

**Every player** must complete this.

1. Every player needs the **One Inch Punch** first. It unlocks when the four soul chests are filled (30 kills each) and is claimed from a **Rituals of the Ancients** board (Gen 1 or Gen 6) — aim at the **bottom-most medal** and press action.
   ![One Inch Punch pickup](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/5/5c/Thunderfist_Pickup_Origins_BOII.png)
2. Go **inside the Excavation Site**. Crusader/Templar zombies now spawn with **white glowing arms**.
   ![Zombie with glowing arms](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/3/3e/Templar_Zombie_with_glowing_arms_Origins_BOII.png)
3. Hit **~20** of them with the One Inch Punch. You only need to *hit* them — the shockwave counts, kills are optional (but killing keeps you alive).
4. A glowing white tablet drops → pick it up → **Iron Fists**.

> ⚠️ **Do not punch Crusaders that are still climbing in through the walls** — the tablet can drop
> out of reach and that makes the rest of the easter egg impossible. Pull them into the open first.

Picking up the tablet emits a white flash (briefly blinds you) and knocks down nearby zombies
(stun only after round 20).

*BO2 bonus:* Iron Fists inherit the element of whatever staff you were holding when you picked up the
tablet — e.g. holding the Fire Staff makes your punches set zombies on fire. (Removed in BO3.)

---

### Step 7: Raise hell

1. Place **all four ultimate staffs** on their matching pedestals **inside the Crazy Place**.
2. Kill **100 Templar zombies inside the Crazy Place**. Their souls stream up into the centre.
3. On completion the screen flashes white and the ceiling turns into a vortex — the portal is open.

![Souls opening the portal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/f/fa/Zombie_souls_opening_the_portal_Origins_BOII.png)

> 🏆 **The trophy/achievement unlocks here.** Step 8 is optional (it's the cutscene + game end).

After this step the falling rocks in the Crazy Place stop, making it a wide-open, Panzer-free,
robot-free training area — genuinely one of the best hold-out spots in the game.

---

### Step 8: Freedom

1. Deploy the **Maxis Drone** in the **centre of the Crazy Place**. He rises into the portal:
   *"Samantha, I must go to her, the paradox must be resolved."*
2. Hold action on the **blue rock in the centre** to "access the teleporter".

![Crazy Place portal open](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/4/45/Crazy_Place_portal_open_Origins_BOII.png)

> ⚠️ **This ends the game.** Do everything you still want to do first (trophies, camo, high round).
> ⚠️ If all six generators weren't on together at some point after Step 5, the teleporter won't fire
> until you re-cap them all.

You get a different ending track, a camera pan into the portal, and a special cutscene after the scoreboard.

## Staff upgrade pattern (all four staffs)

Every staff follows the same four sub-steps. Learn the pattern once:

| # | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | The Crazy Place, your element's area | Solve the elemental **riddle** |
| **B** | The normal map | Complete the **surface task** (after A, a beam of light shoots up from the Excavation Site when B is done) |
| **C** | Lower levels of the Excavation Site | **Ring puzzle**: rotate the four floating rings until *all four* lights are your colour, then shoot the orb inside them with your staff. The orb glows and shoots into the sky. |
| **D** | The Crazy Place | Place your staff on its **elemental pedestal** and kill **~20 zombies** inside (25 for lightning). Pick up your **ultimate staff**. |

**Ring puzzle mechanics (C):** four levers around the lower level rotate the rings — two on the normal
walkway, two on the wooden scaffolding. Each lever turns one ring. Only one element's orb can be
active at a time, so **queue up**: one player does rings, shoots their orb, next player goes.

**Charging (D):** you can leave several staffs on their pedestals at once and farm the Crazy Place —
souls feed every placed staff simultaneously. Combine this with the Fire riddle (see below) to
double-dip.

**If a staff is lost** (you swap it away, bleed out, or disconnect) it returns to its pedestal in the
Excavation Site and **keeps its upgrade** — you never have to redo it.

## Staffs

### Fire — Kagutsuchi's Blood 🔴

Built at the **red pedestal**, lowest level of the Excavation Site.

#### Parts

| # | Location |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Gen 6 Rituals of the Ancients box** — pops open with the part once Generator 6 is powered ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/f/f3/Fire_Staff_Box_Part_Location_Origins_BOII.png) |
| 2 | **Orange glowing plane** — spawns the round *after* the path to the church is opened. Shoot it down; the part lands next to the soul chest closest to the Excavation Site, on the small walkway between two giant footprints ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/e/ec/Fire_Staff_plane_part_Origins_BOII.png) |
| 3 | **Round-8 Panzersoldat** — dropped when the first Panzer dies. **Only source.** Open the No Man's Land door before round 8 or the Panzer won't spawn. |

**Red record:** near the church — on the boxes between the tank and the staircase up (hard to spot if
the tank is parked there), on the church benches opposite the basin, or on boxes next to Generator 6.

#### Tunnel

**Fire tunnel** — to the left of the spawn exit, next to the Rituals of the Ancients box, on the way
to Generator 3. Gramophone + red record → portal → take the **red gem** from the pedestal that opens.

#### Upgrade

- **A — Crazy Place riddle:** kill **~30 zombies with the Fire Staff on the metal grates** in the fire area. Corpses burst into black smoke and fill the four cauldrons nearby.
  ![Fire kill zone](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/9/97/Fire_Staff_Challenge_TCP_Killzone_Origins_BOII.png)
  > 🔁 This is the only riddle that isn't a puzzle — and it doesn't consume souls, so **other staffs can be charging on their pedestals at the same time**. Best value trip in the game.
- **B — Surface task (church):** the **basement** has **seven torches**, each with a number chalked under it (the "4" is a bloodstain, not chalk). **Upstairs, on the far wall**, a set of symbols — **four of them are lit**. Decode those four symbols into numbers and light **those four torches** with the Fire Staff.
  - ⚠️ **The numbers are not 1–7.** Plenty of guides say they are. They aren't. The torches carry specific values — `3` `4` `5` `6` `7` `9` `11` — scattered along the basement walls. Read the number under the torch; don't count torches left to right.
  - **Decoding:** the symbols are ternary (base-3). Read each column of circles: a filled circle = 1, an outline = 0, two filled circles in a column = 2. Rightmost column is the 1s, next left is the 3s, next is the 9s. So `1|1` = 4, `2|0` = 6, `1|0|0` = 9, `1|0|2` = 11.
  - **4 is always one of the four numbers.**
  - All four torches must be **lit at the same time** and the flames are short-lived. Order doesn't matter; if one goes out, relight it.
  - ![Symbol key](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/d/d5/Fire_Staff_Challenge_Symbols_Key_Origins_BOII.png)
  - ![Basement torches](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/1/1d/Fire_Staff_challenge_torches_Origins_BOII.png)
- **C — Rings:** all four rings **red**, shoot the red orb with the Fire Staff.
- **D — Charge:** red pedestal in the Crazy Place, ~20 kills.

**Weapon notes:** fires three horizontal molten rocks. Upgraded it charges (hold fire) for a bigger
blast that leaves burning ground before a secondary explosion. Point-blank vaporises. With **PhD
Flopper** (Der Wunderfizz, BO2 only) diving to prone creates a fire explosion.

### Ice — Ull's Arrow 🔵

Built at the **blue pedestal**, lowest level of the Excavation Site.

#### Parts

All three are **dug up with a shovel from dig spots, and only while it is snowing.**

- It typically snows on **rounds 2–4**, again around **round 10**, then at random.
- One part is in the **starting area** (spawn → workshop, either side), one in the **middle of the map**, one **near the church**.
- ![Ice staff part](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/5/5c/Staff_of_Ice_Part_Origins_BOII.png)

> ⚠️ **The whole squad must protect the snow windows.** Do not end a round while it's snowing until
> the ice player says they're done. Missing the round 2–4 window can cost you 6+ rounds of waiting.

**Blue record:** Tank Station near Generator 2, on one of the shelves.

#### Tunnel

**Ice tunnel** — across from Generator 6, on the left as you exit the church. Gramophone + blue record
→ portal → take the **blue gem**.

#### Upgrade

- **A — Crazy Place riddle:** above the portal float **six stone panels** with base-4 symbols. On the wall there's a **blue ice panel** showing a dot pattern (base-3). Read the current dot pattern, find the floating panel with the matching value, and **shoot that panel with the Ice Staff** — it flips over. Repeat until **all six** are flipped.
  > ⚠️ **Shoot a wrong panel and every flipped panel resets.** Twelve dot patterns exist, only six appear per game — the pairing is randomised, so you must actually read it (or use the key image).
  ![Ice symbol key](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/3/39/Ice_Staff_Challenge_Symbols_Key_Origins_BOII.png)
- **B — Surface task:** three **gravestones with water flowing out of them**. **Freeze each with the Ice Staff, then destroy it with a bullet weapon.**
  1. To the right of the MP40 wall-buy near Generator 4 — ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/8/8b/Ice_Staff_Challenge_Water_Tombstone_1_Origins_BOII.png)
  2. Between the Excavation Site and a giant footprint, close to Generator 4 — ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/1/17/Ice_Staff_Challenge_Water_Tombstone_2_Origins_BOII.png)
  3. On top of the small hill right of the back exit of the Gen 2 bunker, next to the downed mech's hand — ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/1/18/Ice_Staff_Challenge_Water_Tombstone_3_Origins_BOII.png)
- **C — Rings:** all four rings **blue**, shoot the blue orb with the Ice Staff.
- **D — Charge:** blue pedestal in the Crazy Place, ~20 kills.

#### 🩸 Zombie Blood farm (the ice player's superpower)

Shooting **three flaming carts** with the Ice Staff (upgraded or not) spawns a **Zombie Blood** next to
the scaffolding by Pack-a-Punch. **Once per round.** Does not work while it's raining, and if the
carts are shot but the power-up isn't collected, they won't relight that round.

1. Just outside the Excavation Site, by the **MP-40 wall-buy**.
2. By the footprint with the stone chest outside **Generator 5 / Stamin-Up**.
3. Just outside the trenches behind the Excavation Site (the church side), more toward **Generator 4**.

This is how you feed **Step 5**, and how a golden-shovel player finds Empty Perk Bottles.

### Wind — Boreas' Fury 🟡

Built at the **yellow pedestal**, lowest level of the Excavation Site.

#### Parts

All three come from **inside the Giant Robots' heads** — one part per robot (Freya, Odin, Thor).

- **Shoot the glowing foot**, then stand under it and get stepped on → you're teleported into that robot's head. Grab the part.
- Only **one foot per pass is lit**, and it's randomised. Expect several passes.
- ![Wind part inside a robot head](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/b/bb/Wind_Staff_Part_Location_Origins_BOII.png)

> 💡 A **Golden Helmet** (dug with the golden shovel) stops robot feet from downing you — very handy
> for this role and for Step 2/3.

**Yellow record:** near Generator 5 — on the partially broken wall right of Stamin-Up, on the boxes
near the Lightning tunnel entrance, or on the table just inside the Lightning tunnel.

#### Tunnel

**Wind tunnel** — near Generator 4, next to one of the lightning-upgrade panels. Gramophone + yellow
record → portal → take the **yellow gem**.

![Wind portal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/1/19/Wind_Staff_Portal_Origins_BOII.png)

#### Upgrade

- **A — Crazy Place riddle:** above the portal are **four concentric rings**, each carrying four symbols. Each pillar shows a target symbol. Shoot a ring with the Wind Staff to rotate it; align the rings so each row of symbols adds up to the value on the pillar it points at.
  - Symbols are base-4: count the lines in each shape. One shape = its line count. Two shapes = (first × 4) + last. Three shapes = (first × 16) + (second × 4) + last.
  - The correct final arrangement never changes (only the starting rotation is random) — use the key image. Stand between the rocks to the right of the portal gateway for the cleanest view.
  - ![Wind symbol key](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/a/ab/Wind_Staff_Challenge_Symbols_Key_Origins_BOII.png)
- **B — Surface task:** three **smoking stone balls**. Shoot each with the Wind Staff to rotate it until its smoke blows **toward the Excavation Site**.
  1. Behind the **Stamin-Up** machine (Gen 5) — ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/5/5b/Wind_Staff_Part_Challenge_Smoking_Ball_1_Origins_BOII.png)
  2. Between **Generator 4 and the Wind tunnel** — ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/6/66/Wind_Staff_Part_Challenge_Smoking_Ball_2_Origins_BOII.png)
  3. On the **outside section of No Man's Land**, on the tank's return path — ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/4/4a/Wind_Staff_Part_Challenge_Smoking_Ball_3_Origins_BOII.png)
     > Reach #3 by backtracking from the church, or jump off the tank as it returns from the Tank Station, so you can aim the smoke the right way.
- **C — Rings:** all four rings **yellow**, shoot the yellow orb with the Wind Staff.
- **D — Charge:** yellow pedestal in the Crazy Place, ~20 kills.

### Lightning — Kimat's Bite 🟣

Built at the **purple pedestal**, lowest level of the Excavation Site.

#### Parts

All three are in areas you can only reach by **jumping off the tank**. Tank costs **500** to activate
from the control panel on the tank itself (parked under the church near Gen 6); afterwards it can be
called for 500 from the switch at either terminus. It takes **~2 minutes to cool down** at each end —
fewer players riding = faster cooldown.

| Run | Part |
|---|---|
| **Church → Tank Station** | Wooden cut-off staircase **on the right**, just before the trench to Generator 2. ⚠️ Thor can step on the ground here. ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/d/d9/Purple_Staff_Part_Jump_Location_1_Origins_BOII.png) |
| **Tank Station → Church** | Cut-off wooden scaffolding **on the left**, linked to the Excavation Site — leads to a small hole with the part. ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/0/01/Purple_Staff_Part_Jump_Location_2_Origins_BOII.png) |
| **Tank Station → Church** | Dirt path **on the right** just before the church, leading to the church's upper level. ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/7/73/Purple_Staff_Part_Jump_Location_3_Origins_BOII.png) |

> 💡 With two players on the tank you can grab both return-run parts in one trip. Solo it's a
> round-trip per part, roughly.
> 💡 After all three parts are collected, a **random power-up** spawns at each jump-off point once
> per round — a nice reason to keep riding.

**Purple record:** near Generator 4 — inside the tunnel by the Crazy Place gateway, on the bandwagon
to the left as you enter the tunnel, or on the table next to the Der Wunderfizz machine.

#### Tunnel

**Lightning tunnel** — near Generator 5, on the opposite side from the soul chest. Gramophone +
purple record → portal → take the **purple gem**.

#### Upgrade

- **A — Crazy Place riddle:** near the portal, purple triangles on the wall form a **keyboard**. Opposite walls show three chords of three notes each. Play them with the Lightning Staff.
  - **Only the bottom row matters.** Numbering the seven bottom keys **1–7 left to right**, the answer is always: **136 → 357 → 246**.
  - ![Keyboard key](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/b/b5/Lightning_Staff_Keyboard_Key_Origins_BOII.png)
- **B — Surface task:** **seven electrical panels** around the map (an eighth exists but is pre-completed). Turn each knob to the correct direction. After the Crazy Place riddle, panels spark **one at a time, in this exact order**:
  1. Right next to **Generator 5** → point **down** ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/7/79/Staff_of_Lightning_panel_Origins_BOII.png)
  2. **Church basement**, next to the torch numbered **3** → point **right**
  3. **Church upstairs**, next to the repairable window → point **up**
  4. Next to the **Wind tunnel**, right of its entrance → point **up**
  5. **Spawn room**, at the bottom of the stairs → point **left**
  6. **Tank Station** (Gen 2), left of the back door → point **down**
  7. **Behind the Excavation Site**, next to the path toward the church → point **up**
  > 💡 You can pre-turn these **at any point in the game** as you walk past — just remember how far each
  > knob is turned. Doing this during rounds 1–6 saves a huge amount of running later.
- **C — Rings:** all four rings **purple**, shoot the purple orb with the Lightning Staff.
- **D — Charge:** purple pedestal in the Crazy Place, **25 kills** (slightly more than the others).

## Air strike (G-Strike Beacon)

### How to get it

Each player who wants one runs their own tablet. There are **four dirty stone tablets** at the Tank
Station, so **all four players can do this in parallel**.

1. **Tank Station (Gen 2)** — pick up a **dirty stone tablet** (no prompt appears; just press action on it).
2. Carry it to the **church** and put it in the **basin of holy water**.
3. Kill **20 zombies with melee** near the basin to cleanse it, then pick the tablet back up.
4. Carry it back to the **table in the Tank Station — without stepping in mud.** Step in mud and you must return to the church and re-cleanse it (just press action on the basin).
5. Place it on the table, then kill **20 more zombies with melee** near it. The **G-Strike Beacon** appears.

**Notes**
- You get 2 beacons (3 in BO3). A **Max Ammo** refills them.
- If you lose them (down, or traded for Monkey Bombs) you can get them back from the **Mystery Box**.
- Do this **early** — 40 melee kills at round 20+ is miserable.
- Take the **muddy tank road** carefully; the safe route is along the wooden beams/planks.

### What it's used for

- **Step 3: Rain fire** — thrown on the seal near Generator 5 to call the robot artillery. **Mandatory.**
- Crowd control: attracts zombies then a robot missile deletes them. Doesn't work indoors/roofed
  (Crazy Place, Hall of the Ancients) — no line of fire from the sky.
- Stops **generator attacks** if thrown at a generator under attack.
- Excellent against the **10 Panzers** in Step 4.

## Super Fist (One Inch Punch → Iron Fists)

### How to get it

Fill the **four soul chests** (30 kills near each). Any player's kills count; a chest disappears when full.

**Chest locations** (all in giant footprints):
1. Footprint near **Generator 5 / Stamin-Up**
2. Footprint on the path from the **Excavation Site to the church** (the only one not in Odin's path)
3. Footprint on the path from the **No Man's Land entrance to Generator 4 / Juggernog**
4. Footprint in the **map corner near Generator 4 / Juggernog / MP-40 wall-buy**

> ⚠️ **If a robot steps on a chest before it's full, it closes and its progress resets.** Chest #2 is
> the safe one. Watch robot lanes.
> ⚠️ Zombies spawned from **dig spots do not count** toward chests.

When all four are full, go to a **Rituals of the Ancients** board (Generator 1 or Generator 6), aim at
the **bottom-most medal**, press action → **One Inch Punch**.

*If you bleed out and lose it, or you weren't in the game when the chests finished, just take it from
a Rituals board again.*

### What it's used for

- **Step 6: Wield a fist of iron** — every player must upgrade theirs to **Iron Fists** (~20 punches on white-glowing Crusaders inside the Excavation Site).
- One-hit melee kill until round 19 (round 36 once upgraded).
- Great for the 40 melee kills the **G-Strike** needs — get the Punch before the beacons if you can.

## Maxis Drone

### Parts

| Part | Locations |
|---|---|
| **Brain** | Always: on a table on the **lower floor of the starting room**, next to the stairs up to Generator 1 |
| **Frame** | One of: **Ice tunnel** near Gen 6, in front of the Crazy Place portal · near the **crossbone sign** on the tank's muddy path to **Gen 4** · near the **crossbone sign** on the tank's muddy path to **Gen 5** |
| **Rotor** | One of: near the **very bottom of the Excavation Site**, on a box on scaffolding next to a lever · **top of the Excavation Site near Pack-a-Punch** · in the Excavation Site around the **secret entrance** |

Build at a workbench (the church bench is the usual one).

### How it works

Deployed, it flies around you for **~90 seconds** shooting zombies with an **MG08/15**, grabbing
power-ups and reviving downed players. Then it returns to the workbench to recharge and can't be
picked up until it's ready.

*In BO3 you can carry the drone **or** Trip Mines, not both.*

### Unleashing the horde

**Step 4** — deploy it right next to the broken seal near Gen 5. It won't return to its station until
you recover it in **Step 5**.

### Getting the discs (free upgraded MG08/15 → Magna Collider)

While holding the drone, stand near each of these four **small yellow discs** — the drone flies over
and collects it. Collect all four and the drone drops a **Magna Collider** (Pack-a-Punched MG08/15) in
front of the Pack-a-Punch machine, free for anyone to take.

1. On the **destroyed roof beside Generator 5**.
2. Just outside the spawn-room door toward Generator 2, in the trench hole with flaming debris — the disc is at the far end.
3. On the **roof of the broken church**, on the tank path heading toward Generator 4.
4. On top of the **scaffold above the Excavation Site entrance closest to the church**.

## Other stuff

### Shovels

#### Where to get them

Four exist. Two are always in the **spawn room**:
- Leaning by the **radio where you spawn**.
- In the small corridor between a barrier and the purchasable door to **Generator 3**.

The other two spawn at any two of:
- In the **church**, at the rear of the tank near the treads.
- In the tunnel leading to the **Wind tunnel** (Generator 4).
- In the tunnel leading to the **Lightning tunnel** (Generator 5).

#### What they're used for

Digging dig spots. Possible results: **Ice Staff parts (only while snowing)**, Zombie Blood, Blood
Money, Mauser C96, Remington 870, Ballista, M1216, DSR 50 — and the bad ones: a zombie, one live
grenade, or two live grenades. Rare: Max Ammo, Double Points, Fire Sale, Insta-Kill, Nuke.

#### Golden shovel (and helmet)

**Dig 30 spots in a game** → your shovel turns golden. It unlocks better digs (KSG, AK74u, STG-44,
MP-40, Claymores, and the **Golden Helmet**) and lowers the chance of bad results.

- **Golden Helmet**: stops the **Giant Robots' feet from downing you**. Excellent for the Wind player and for Steps 2/3.
- With the golden shovel **and Zombie Blood active**, you can see **glowing orange dig spots** that give an **Empty Perk Bottle** — a 5th perk slot. Combine with the ice player's Zombie Blood farm.

### Shield (Zombie Shield)

Optional but strong for reviving and for surviving the Panzer step.

| Part | Locations |
|---|---|
| **Face Screen** | Fire tunnel, on a table right of the gateway · right of the fire tunnel in the trench room · trench tunnel near Speed Cola, behind the crate heading to the workshop |
| **Handles** | Right of the first area leading to Gen 2 from spawn, in the trench room · end of the trenches past Gen 2 and the Workshop · near Gen 2 on top of the Tank Station by broken piping |
| **Shield Frame** | Near Gen 4, left of the nearby MP40 · in the marshland just outside Gen 4 heading to the church trenches · in another giant robot footprint near the Excavation Site |

Blocks damage front (held) or back (stowed), can be planted as an obstacle in BO2, and deflects your
own explosives. Breaks with use; rebuild at the bench where you made it.

### Upgraded MG08/15 (Maxis Drone + discs)

See [Getting the discs](#getting-the-discs-free-upgraded-mg0815--magna-collider) above — free
Pack-a-Punched LMG, well worth the detour before the Panzer step.

### Rituals of the Ancients (free rewards)

Four objectives; spend the tokens at the boards by **Generator 1** or **Generator 6**:

| Objective | Reward |
|---|---|
| Spend 30,000 points | **Double Tap Root Beer** (can't take it if you already have Double Tap) |
| 115 **normal**-zombie headshots (Templars don't count) | Pack-a-Punched **Galil / STG-44 / SCAR-H** |
| Capture all 6 generators | **Max Ammo** |
| Fill 4 soul chests | **One Inch Punch** |

Completing all four in one game is also the *Overachiever* trophy.

### Perks & machines

- **Quick Revive** — spawn (Gen 1)
- **Speed Cola** — Generator 3
- **Juggernog** — Generator 4
- **Stamin-Up** — Generator 5
- **Mule Kick** — inside the Excavation Site, across from the staff pedestals
- **Der Wunderfizz** — random perk for 1500, including **Double Tap, PhD Flopper (BO2 only), Deadshot Daiquiri, Electric Cherry**. The active machine is the one being struck by lightning.
- **Pack-a-Punch** — top of the Excavation Site, needs **all 6 generators**.

### Sound / music easter eggs (optional, free fun)

- **Shepherd of Fire** (Avenged Sevenfold) — three red radios: on the scaffolding in the Excavation Site (above a Maxis Drone rotor spot), inside the robot **Freya** opposite the audio log, and on the left edge of the **fire area in the Crazy Place**.
- **Archangel** — three green Divinium blocks: spawn room right of the Rituals chest behind the bunk bed, second floor of the workshop under a bed opposite the stairs, and on the scaffolding left of the Excavation Site entrance facing spawn.
- **Aether** — go prone and press action at both "1" floor panels at Generator 1 and the "5" at Generator 5 (Gen 5 must be powered).

## General Notes (MUST READ)

- **Shovels:** whoever builds the **Ice Staff** must have a shovel. All ice parts need digging.
- **Snow:** it snows on some rounds. **If it is snowing, do not end the round unless the ice player says so.** Snow is roughly rounds 2–4, then ~round 10, then random.
- **Don't end rounds unilaterally.** Always confirm with the squad first.
- **Don't grab Nukes** — a Nuke wipes the map and can end the round before someone finished a step. (Power-ups drop at random; the problem is picking it up, not how you killed.) Same logic for **Insta-Kill** if someone is farming soul-chest or melee kills.
- **Melee is required**, not forbidden: the G-Strike needs 40 melee kills and Step 6 needs ~20 punches. Get the **One Inch Punch** early to make that fast.
- **Guns:** keep and Pack-a-Punch the **Mauser C96** (starting pistol) — the upgraded version is genuinely good. Buy the **MP40** as a second gun early (wall-buy at the **Tank Station / Gen 2**, and another near **Generator 4**). Both fall off hard past round 20 — by then you should be on ultimate staffs, a Pack-a-Punched LMG, or the Ray Gun Mark II.
- **Panzersoldat** spawns first on **round 8** (only if the No Man's Land door is open), then every 3–5 rounds. Shoot the **red light on its claw arm** to free a grabbed teammate. It cannot be distracted by Monkey Bombs or G-Strikes.
- **Giant robots** instantly down you if stepped on — unless you have the **Golden Helmet**.
- **Generator defence:** from round 10, keep an ear out for Maxis announcing an attack. Losing a generator kills Pack-a-Punch and can block Steps 1, 2 and 8.
- **Staffs never break.** Lose one and it returns to its Excavation Site pedestal, upgrade intact.
- **Step 7 gives the trophy. Step 8 ends the game.** Do your camo/high-round/trophy business before Step 8.
