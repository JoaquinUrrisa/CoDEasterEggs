Quick Percent Math

Test Formula Example
Easy (score ÷ 10) × 100 7/10 = 70% → Veteran
Medium (score ÷ 12) × 100 8/12 ≈ 67% → Hardened
Hard (score ÷ 15) × 100 14/15 ≈ 93% → Realism

Squad Clearance Checklist

  • Everyone agrees on Richtofen or Maxis before round 1
  • Lobby is on Original difficulty
  • At least one Realism/Veteran player owns the step order
  • Recruit players have one clear job written above
  • Answer key is off the table
  • EMP plan / Turbine plan is spoken out loud once

Optional House Rules

Lowest rank picks bus music complaints only. Lowest scorer buys the first box spin (honor system). Any Realism player who downs to lava before power owes the squad a Turbine ferry. If two players tie for Realism, they split Samuel duty / countdown duty.
