# 🟡 Wind Staff Player Guide (P4)

A pruned copy of the [full guide](../index.html), focused only on what the **Wind Staff** player needs.

**Your zone:** the middle of the map / No Man's Land — wherever the robots are walking.
**Your staff:** Staff of Wind → **Boreas' Fury**.
**Your extra jobs:** you are the **gramophone carrier**. Everyone's staff depends on you running the
portal circuit, so treat that as your first priority once the map opens up.

> ⚠️ Your parts come from **inside the three giant robots**, and only **one foot per pass is lit** at
> random. This is a patience job — start watching robot passes from round 1 and grab whatever comes up.

## Job #1: the gramophone

**The gramophone always spawns on the floor of the Excavation Site main chamber.** Only one player can
carry it, and it's the key to five different things — so it's yours.

### Five records

| Record | Opens | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Black** | **Lower levels of the Excavation Site** (staff pedestals + rings) | Outside the Excavation Site: next to the excavation sign · in the wheelbarrow by Pack-a-Punch · in the trench near the church debris |
| **Red** | Fire portal | Near the church: boxes between the tank and the staircase up · church benches opposite the basin · boxes by Gen 6 |
| **Yellow** | Wind portal | Near Gen 5: broken wall right of Stamin-Up · boxes by the Lightning tunnel entrance · table just inside the Lightning tunnel |
| **Purple** | Lightning portal | Near Gen 4: inside the tunnel by the gateway · bandwagon on the left as you enter · table by Der Wunderfizz |
| **Blue** | Ice portal | Tank Station (Gen 2), on a shelf |

> **Do the black record first.** Nobody can build any staff until the lower levels are open.

### The circuit

Go in map order so you're not backtracking:

1. **Fire tunnel** — left of the spawn exit, by the Rituals box, on the way to Gen 3 → P1 takes the red gem
2. **Wind tunnel** — near Generator 4, next to a lightning panel → **you** take the yellow gem
3. **Lightning tunnel** — near Generator 5, opposite the soul chest → P3 takes the purple gem
4. **Ice tunnel** — across from Generator 6, left as you exit the church → P2 takes the blue gem

![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/1/19/Wind_Staff_Portal_Origins_BOII.png)

**How it works:** place the gramophone on the table at the end of the tunnel while holding that
element's record. The gateway builds itself, then **run straight into it**. The portal falls apart
after ~15 seconds.

**Key rules:**
- **A gem can only be taken if you entered through that element's own tunnel.** So all four trips are needed.
- Once a player has used a portal once, **they can rebuild that portal from inside the Crazy Place**
  without the gramophone — so later Crazy Place trips (riddles, charging, Step 7) don't need you.
- Announce each trip. Everyone should come along; it's four free portal unlocks per person.

## Build the Staff of Wind

Built at the **yellow pedestal**, lowest level of the Excavation Site.

### Parts — one inside each giant robot

The three robots are **Freya** (left, church side), **Odin** (centre, Excavation Site) and **Thor**
(right, over Gens 2 & 3). Each head holds one part.

**How to get inside:**
1. Watch a robot approach. **One foot per pass has glowing lights under it** — and which foot is randomised.
2. **Shoot the glowing foot.**
3. Stand under it and **let it step on you** → you're teleported into that robot's head.
4. Grab the part, then eject (or ride out the purge).

![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/b/bb/Wind_Staff_Part_Location_Origins_BOII.png)

> ⚠️ A robot foot that you **haven't** shot **instantly downs you**.
> 💡 The **Golden Helmet** (dug by a golden shovel — ask the ice player) makes robot feet harmless.
> This is the single best quality-of-life item for your role, and it also helps in Steps 2 and 3.
> 💡 While inside Freya, there's a **red radio** opposite the audio log — one of the three for the
> *Shepherd of Fire* music easter egg. Free, takes a second.

### Wind tunnel → yellow gem

**Near Generator 4**, next to one of the lightning-upgrade panels. Gramophone + **yellow record** →
portal → run through. The **yellow pedestal** opens and the wind gem rises out.

![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/8/8c/Wind_Elemental_Gem_Pedestal_Origins_BOII.png)

Then build at the **yellow pedestal** on the lowest Excavation level.

## Upgrade to Boreas' Fury

### A — Crazy Place riddle (the concentric rings)

Above the wind portal are **four concentric rings**, each carrying four symbols. Each **pillar** shows
a target symbol. **Shoot a ring with the Wind Staff to rotate it.** Align the rings so that each row
of symbols adds up to the value on the pillar it points at.

**Reading the symbols (base 4):** count the lines in each shape.
- One shape → its line count
- Two shapes → (first × 4) + last
- Three shapes → (first × 16) + (second × 4) + last

![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/a/ab/Wind_Staff_Challenge_Symbols_Key_Origins_BOII.png)

> 💡 **The correct final arrangement never changes** — only the starting rotation is randomised. You can
> just match the key image instead of doing the maths.
> 💡 Best vantage point: stand **between the rocks to the right of the portal gateway**.

When it's right, the inner rings rotate up with an audio cue.

### B — The three smoking stone balls

Three stone balls emit smoke. **Shoot each with the Wind Staff to rotate it until its smoke blows
toward the Excavation Site.**

1. **Behind the Stamin-Up machine** (Generator 5)
   ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/5/5b/Wind_Staff_Part_Challenge_Smoking_Ball_1_Origins_BOII.png)
2. **Between Generator 4 and the Wind tunnel**
   ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/6/66/Wind_Staff_Part_Challenge_Smoking_Ball_2_Origins_BOII.png)
3. **On the outside section of No Man's Land**, on the tank's return path
   ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/callofduty/images/4/4a/Wind_Staff_Part_Challenge_Smoking_Ball_3_Origins_BOII.png)

> ⚠️ **Ball #3 must be approached from the right side** so you can push the smoke the correct way.
> Either backtrack the tank path from the **church**, or **jump off the tank** as it returns from the
> Tank Station. Coordinate with the Lightning player's tank run.

Audio cue + beam of light from the Excavation Site when all three are aimed correctly.

### C — Ring puzzle (Excavation Site, lower level)

Four floating rings, four levers (two on the walkway, two on the wooden scaffolding). Turn them until
**all four ring lights are yellow**, then **shoot the yellow orb with the Wind Staff**.

> ⚠️ One element at a time — queue with your teammates.

### D — Charge in the Crazy Place

Place the staff on the **yellow pedestal** in the Crazy Place, kill **~20 zombies** inside, pick up
**Boreas' Fury**.

> 💡 Do this during the Fire player's grate farm — their riddle doesn't consume souls, so your staff
> charges off their kills.

## Your weapon

Fires a wind blast that flings zombies away and kills them on impact. Upgraded (**Boreas' Fury**) it
**holds to charge** into a vortex that sucks in and shreds a group — one of the best panic buttons in
the game. Melee one-hit-kills until round 16. Press left on the D-pad for **Sekhmet's Vigor** to revive
a downed teammate at range.

If you lose the staff it returns to its Excavation Site pedestal **with the upgrade intact**.

## Shared jobs (everyone does these)

### G-Strike beacon — do it early

1. **Tank Station (Gen 2)** — take a **dirty stone tablet** (no prompt, just press action). Four exist, one per player.
2. Carry it to the **church basin of holy water**, insert it.
3. **20 melee kills** near the basin → take the cleansed tablet.
4. Return it to the **Tank Station table without stepping in mud** (use the wooden beams). Muddy = re-cleanse at the basin.
5. **20 more melee kills** near the table → **G-Strike Beacon**.

### Soul chests → One Inch Punch

Four chests, 30 kills each, in giant footprints:
Gen 5 / Stamin-Up · Excavation→church path (safe, not in Odin's lane) · No Man's Land entrance→Gen 4 · map corner by Gen 4 / MP-40.
Claim at a **Rituals of the Ancients** board (Gen 1 or Gen 6) — aim at the **bottom-most medal**.

> ⚠️ **You're the robot player — you'll see this happen.** If a robot steps on an unfinished chest it
> closes and resets. Call out robot lanes so the squad farms the safe chest first.

### Easter egg steps — your part

- **Step 2:** **Boreas' Fury goes into Odin** — the **centre** robot, the one that treads the Excavation Site. You're already the robot expert, so you'll likely be placing more than one staff. Remember: **Ice → Freya (left/church), Wind → Odin (centre), Lightning → Thor (right/Gens 2&3), Fire → staff room pedestal.**
- **Step 3:** you're the natural **button presser** — enter a robot, press the red button, while a teammate throws the **G-Strike onto the seal behind/right of Generator 5**. Count them down before you press. After Step 2, all three robots roam at once and only one foot is lit at a time, so expect a few attempts.
- **Step 6:** you need your own **Iron Fists** — ~20 One Inch Punch hits on white-glowing Crusaders inside the Excavation Site. Don't punch ones still climbing through walls.
- **Step 7:** Boreas' Fury on its Crazy Place pedestal, help kill 100 Templars inside.

## Reminders

- **Black record first** — the whole squad is blocked until the lower Excavation levels are open.

- Don't grab **Nukes**.

- Ask the ice player for a **Golden Helmet** as soon as they have a golden shovel.
