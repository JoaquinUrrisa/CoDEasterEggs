Tranzit Easter Egg

A complete, beginner-friendly walkthrough of Tower of Babble — the main Easter Egg on TranZit. Use this on a phone or tablet next to your game: pick a path, check off steps as you go, and jump to part locations when you get stuck.

Reward Tower of Babble

Choose your path

TranZit has two different Easter Egg endings. Your team must agree on one before power is settled. You cannot freely switch mid-run after committing.

Maxis path

  • Easier and shorter
  • Minimum 2 players (4 is smoother)
  • Turn power ON, then OFF
  • Need Turbines + EMP grenades
  • Kill the Avogadro under the pylon
  • Completion color: orange

Richtofen path

  • Longer, more coordination
  • 4 players strongly recommended
  • Keep power ON
  • Someone should play as Samuel
  • Build & destroy Jet Gun, then EMP 4 lamps
  • Completion color: blue

Before you start

Read this once before loading the map. Everything below assumes you have never done a TranZit Easter Egg before.

Hard requirements

  • Original difficulty only. The Easter Egg will not complete on easier custom settings that disable it.
  • Online / local co-op as required by your path. Maxis needs at least two players on console/vanilla. Richtofen is designed for four.
  • Agree on the path. Maxis wants power off. Richtofen wants power on. Mixing steps soft-locks the wrong ending.
  • Mystery Box luck. Both paths need EMP Grenades from the box. Richtofen also needs an explosive weapon (Ray Gun recommended).
Do not skip the path decision

If one player turns power off for Maxis while others are building the Jet Gun for Richtofen, you will waste a lot of time. Decide in the lobby.

What you will hear

On the Maxis path, Dr. Maxis speaks over the PA / radio after power events. On the Richtofen path, Richtofen speaks only to Samuel Stuhlinger (the character with the green eyes / who ate zombie flesh in the comics). If nobody is Samuel, your team may still do the steps, but you lose the voice cues that confirm progress.

Quick glossary

Turbine
Portable generator buildable at Bus Depot. Powers doors, lampposts (when main power is off), and is required for Maxis.
Pylon
Huge electrical tower in the cornfield between Farm and Power Station. Main Easter Egg site.
Avogadro
Electric boss that appears after power is turned on (and can be re-spawned with storms). Looks like a humanoid made of electricity.
Denizen
Small creature in the fog that jumps on your head. Under a green lamppost it digs a teleport hole.
EMP Grenades
Special grenades from the Mystery Box. Disable electronics and are used to kill Avogadro / hit lampposts.
Jet Gun
Buildable wonder weapon (Thrustodyne Aeronautics Model 23). Required only for Richtofen’s path.
Navcard Table
Buildable table under the pylon. Needed for the overall Victis storyline setup; build it if it is not already there.
Green lampposts
Street lamps that glow green when powered. Used for Denizen teleports and the final Easter Egg steps.

Know the map

TranZit is a large map connected by a looping bus and dangerous fog. Learn the stop order so you never wonder “where do I go next?”

Bus route (main stops)

1

Bus Depot (spawn)

Starting area. Build the Turbine here. Navcard and meteor part nearby. Safe early lamppost for finals.

T

Tunnel (between Depot and Diner)

Fog/road stretch with the M16 wall-buy. Jet Gun engine and sometimes the Navcard wooden board spawn here.

2

Diner

Restaurant / gas station area. Garage can hold the Navcard radio. Lamppost outside is great for final steps.

3

Farm

House and barn. Fridge area can hold a Navcard power-box part. Good points / camping for box hits.

C

Cornfield — Pylon & Nacht

Left / road side: the big pylon (Easter Egg tower). Right paths: Nacht der Untoten prototype building (Jet Gun handle / radio spawn).

4

Power Station

Enter via the outhouse hatch into the lab. Build and flip the power switch here. Jet Gun wires are on the lava/catwalk side after power.

H

Hunter’s Cabin

In the fog between Power Station and Town. Jet Gun pressure gauge and Bowie Knife live here.

5

Town

Final bus stop. Bar workbench builds the Jet Gun (near Stamin-Up). Bank, Pack-a-Punch after power, lava streets.

Fog survival tip

Denizens attack in fog. Melee them off your head quickly. Lava on the road damages you — jump over cracks. The bus is slower but safer for long trips.

Shared setup

Do these first on either path. Check items off as you finish them. Details for every part spawn are also in Locations.

Setup 1

Build the Turbine at Bus Depot

The Turbine is a portable generator. On Maxis it is mandatory for multiple steps. On Richtofen it is still extremely useful for doors and teleports.

What to do

  1. Stay in the Bus Depot starting room / nearby interior.
  2. Find all 3 Turbine parts (fan, mannequin/body, and tail/vane pieces). They always spawn inside the depot starting area.
  3. Take them to the workbench in the depot and build the Turbine.
  4. Place the Turbine next to the powered door near the workbench to open both exits without buying the main door.
  5. Pick the Turbine back up before leaving. You carry it as equipment.
Team tip

On Maxis with 2–4 players, every player should eventually grab their own Turbine from the same workbench (parts respawn for each builder). Fresh Turbines matter — damaged ones fail steps.

Setup 2

Build the Navcard Table under the pylon

If the table is already built on your account/profile under the cornfield pylon, you can skip collecting parts. First-time / fresh accounts usually need to build it.

Navcard (optional but recommended)

  • Behind the Bus Depot, check behind a dumpster for the Navcard pickup.
  • A small card icon appears near your score when you have it.
  • Inserting it later may say it is the “wrong” card — that is normal for TranZit’s card. The table still needs to exist.

Carry each part to the pylon workbench

  • Meteor (115 rock) — Bus Depot, in a crevice / zombie barrier near the rear area by the Mystery Box side.
  • Wooden board / table — Tunnel by the M16, or Power Station catwalk edge under Tombstone.
  • Radio — Diner garage locker top, or Nacht der Untoten table.
  • Electrical / power box — Farm fridge area, or Town dumpster near upper Mystery Box.

You can only carry one buildable part at a time. Place each under the pylon, then fetch the next.

Setup 3

Reach Power Station and build the power switch
  1. Ride the bus: Depot → Diner → Farm → Power Station.
  2. Buy the hatch/outhouse door and drop into the underground lab.
  3. Collect the three power parts in that facility:
    • Zombie hand / arm
    • Lever / switch handle
    • Circuit / power panel
  4. Build them on the power workbench and prepare to flip the switch.
Path split happens here

Maxis: turn power ON, wait for Maxis to finish talking, then turn it OFF.
Richtofen: turn power ON and leave it ON for the rest of the egg.

Turning power on the first time also introduces the Avogadro. Clear him or run as needed, then continue your chosen path below.

Path walkthroughs

Switch between paths anytime. Your checklist progress is saved separately for Maxis and Richtofen in this browser.

Maxis overview

  1. Power ON → wait → power OFF
  2. Get EMP Grenades from the Mystery Box
  3. At the pylon: place Turbines, spawn Avogadro, EMP him
  4. Power two green lampposts with Turbines (keep pylon Turbines up in 4-player)
Maxis 1

Turn power ON, then OFF after Maxis speaks
  1. Flip the power switch ON in the Power Station lab.
  2. Wait until Dr. Maxis finishes his dialogue about the power interfering with communication.
  3. Flip the switch again to turn power OFF.
  4. Power-off seals the lab door — place a Turbine to open it, pick the Turbine up, and leave.
Perks will die

With power off, electric perk machines stop working and purchased electric perks fail. Buy essential gear before committing, or accept running without them. Tombstone / strategy around this.

Maxis 2

Get EMP Grenades from the Mystery Box

At least one player needs EMP Grenades. Follow the blue light in the sky to the current Mystery Box location.

  • Do not waste EMPs on random electronics before the Avogadro step.
  • Save points; this can take many spins.
Maxis 3

Place two Turbines under the cornfield pylon
  1. Travel on foot into the cornfield between Farm and Power Station.
  2. Find the large metal pylon / transmission tower (Navcard Table is underneath).
  3. Have two players place working Turbines in separate corners under the pylon (often described as either side of the Navcard Table / near a back leg).
  4. Keep zombies from destroying the Turbines.
Damaged Turbine?

If it is almost broken, let zombies finish it and rebuild a fresh one at Bus Depot. Do not risk the EMP step on a dying Turbine.

Maxis 4

Spawn Avogadro during the storm, then EMP him

Spawn

  1. Stay under the pylon and survive rounds.
  2. Wait until lightning / thunderstorm is heavily active above your area (storm all around, not a distant flash).
  3. End the round (kill the last zombie) while that storm is overhead so Avogadro spawns.

Kill with EMP

  1. Confirm both Turbines are still running.
  2. Lure Avogadro directly underneath the pylon.
  3. Throw an EMP at Avogadro — not onto your Turbines (EMPs disable them).
  4. He should launch upward into the tower. Maxis will speak if it worked.
Safe EMP angle

Keep Turbines on one side of the tower and pull Avogadro to the opposite side before you throw.

Maxis 5

Power two green lampposts with Turbines

After Maxis confirms the Avogadro step, send energy from lampposts to the pylon.

2-player lobby

  1. Pick up your Turbines from the pylon.
  2. Each player goes to a different green lamppost.
  3. Easiest pair: Bus Depot lamppost + Diner lamppost.
  4. Place a Turbine under each lamppost at the same time.

4-player lobby (recommended)

  1. Leave two Turbines running under the pylon.
  2. The other two players place Turbines under two different lampposts (Depot + Diner are safest).

Success look: lampposts glow with an orange orb / energy and send a beam toward the pylon. In the lobby afterward, TranZit’s icon is highlighted orange.

Richtofen overview

  1. Keep power ON (Samuel hears Richtofen)
  2. Build the Jet Gun in Town
  3. Overheat / destroy Jet Gun under the pylon
  4. ~25 explosive kills under the pylon
  5. All four players EMP four different green lampposts together
Richtofen 1

Turn power ON and leave it on
  1. Flip the Power Station switch ON.
  2. Do not turn it back off.
  3. Confirm someone is playing as Samuel Stuhlinger so Richtofen’s lines can confirm steps.

With power on, perks and Pack-a-Punch are available — use that advantage; this path is longer.

Richtofen 2

Build the Jet Gun at the Town bar

Official name: Thrustodyne Aeronautics Model 23. Build at the workbench in the Town bar (near Stamin-Up). One part per trip.

  • Jet Engine — Tunnel between Bus Depot and Diner (near M16 / cars / garage corner).
  • Wires — Power Station lava/catwalk area after power (barrels, ledge, broken electrical box).
  • Pressure Gauge — Hunter’s Cabin (fireplace, bed frame, or barrel).
  • Handle / handbrake — Nacht der Untoten in the cornfield (stairs, cabinets, shelf, lamp).

Split the map among teammates so four people can deliver parts faster.

Richtofen 3

Get EMP Grenades and an explosive weapon
  • EMP Grenades: ideally all four players (final step).
  • Explosive kills weapon: original Ray Gun is best. Pack-a-Punched pistols (Mustang & Sally) also work but can down you — TranZit has no PhD Flopper.
  • Ray Gun Mark II does not count the same way for this explosive step — use the original Ray Gun.
Richtofen 4

Destroy the Jet Gun under the pylon
  1. Bring the team under the cornfield pylon / Navcard Table.
  2. Equip the Jet Gun, aim up at the structure, and hold the trigger.
  3. Do not release when it overheats — keep firing until the gun fully breaks apart and parts fall on the ground.
  4. Samuel should hear Richtofen confirm. If Richtofen says the pylon needs to be hotter, rebuild the Jet Gun and repeat.
Richtofen 5

Get ~25 explosive kills under the pylon
  1. Stay underneath the pylon.
  2. Kill about 25 zombies with explosive damage in that area.
  3. Continue until Richtofen tells Samuel the power is becoming too strong / too much.
Train carefully

Ray Gun splash is safer than Mustang & Sally at point-blank. Keep distance so you do not down yourself mid-count.

Richtofen 6

EMP four different green lampposts at once
  1. Assign each of the four players a different green lamppost before you start.
  2. Good easy posts: Bus Depot, Diner, and other green lamps along the road/fog between major stops (Power area, cornfield approaches, Town/bridge approaches).
  3. Clear zombies around each thrower.
  4. On a shared countdown (3…2…1…), all four throw EMPs so the lamps disable in the same short window.

Success look: blue electrical energy from the lamps / blue beam at the pylon. Lobby icon highlights blue.

Fewer than 4 players

Still need four lamp hits in a tight window. A common method:

  1. At lamp A, place a Turbine (if needed) and get a Denizen on your head so it digs a portal under the lamp.
  2. EMP lamp A, immediately jump through the portal, EMP lamp B on arrival.
  3. Coordinate the other players on the remaining lamps in the same window.

This is inconsistent. Four players is strongly preferred.

Troubleshooting & tips

Maxis never speaks / power-off did nothing

You must wait for Maxis to finish his first speech after turning power ON before you turn it OFF. Flipping too early can fail to register. Also confirm you are on Original difficulty.

EMP killed Avogadro but the step failed

Both Turbines must be active under the pylon. Avogadro must be under the tower. Your EMP may have disabled the Turbines — wait for them to recover or replace them, then try on a later Avogadro spawn.

Richtofen is silent

Only Samuel hears him. Have a teammate select Samuel. Others can still perform physical steps, but they will not get voice confirmation.

Jet Gun step will not complete

You must fully destroy the gun under the pylon by holding fire through overheat until parts scatter. Merely overheating briefly or swapping weapons does not count. If told the pylon needs to be hotter, rebuild and repeat.

Explosive kills are not counting

Stay under the pylon. Use the original Ray Gun or Mustang & Sally. Do not rely on Ray Gun Mark II. Continue until Samuel hears the “too much power” style line.

Final Richtofen EMPs failed

Four different lampposts must be hit in a very short window. Use a countdown, do not double-target one lamp, and clear zombies first so nobody is interrupted mid-throw.

How do Denizen teleports work?

When a lamppost is green, let a Denizen jump on your head and stand under the lamp. It digs a portal. Dive in to warp to another lamp. Useful for map travel and for covering two EMP lamps with one player.

How do I know we finished?

Maxis: orange energy to the pylon; TranZit icon outlined orange in the menu. Richtofen: blue energy / blue pylon effect; icon outlined blue. You also unlock the Tower of Babble achievement/trophy when eligible.
