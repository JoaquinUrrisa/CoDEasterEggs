Interactive companion guide · squad of four

Little Lost Girl

Free Samantha from Agartha. Four players, four elemental staffs, one dig site in Northern France that gets exponentially harder every round. This guide splits the whole Easter Egg into four parallel jobs so nothing sits waiting its turn.

8 steps 4 staffs 6 generators ~2–3 hours Silver trophy

What you are actually doing

  1. Secure the keys — build and upgrade all four staffs
  2. Ascend from darkness — staffs into the robot pedestals
  3. Rain fire — robot button plus a G-Strike on the seal
  4. Unleash the horde — ten Panzersoldats
  5. Skewer the winged beast — the Zombie Blood pilot
  6. Wield a fist of iron — everyone gets Iron Fists
  7. Raise hell — 100 souls, and the trophy pops
  8. Freedom — ends the game

The two hard clocks

Snow Every Ice Staff part comes from a dig spot, and dig spots only give parts while it is snowing. Snow lands around rounds 2–4, again near round 10, then at random. Nobody ends a round while it is snowing.
Round 8 The first Panzersoldat drops a Fire Staff part and it is the only source. It only spawns if the No Man's Land door is already open, so open it before round 8.

Written for Black Ops II. The Black Ops III Zombies Chronicles version plays the same — differences are listed under Rules.

Pick your staff

Each player owns one staff and one zone. Choose yours and the guide will tag your jobs wherever they appear, and open your staff panel by default.

The dig site

The Excavation Site sits in the middle of everything. The church is west, spawn and the Tank Station are east, and Generators 4 and 5 flank the mound in No Man's Land.

Generators

200 points per player — 800 in a four-man. Whoever pays gets it back on capture; everyone on the pad gets +100. All six live opens Pack-a-Punch.

Defend them From round 10, Templars periodically attack a live generator. Losing one kills Pack-a-Punch and blocks steps 1, 2 and 8. Killing the attackers drops a Max Ammo, and a Monkey Bomb or G-Strike thrown in the area stops the attack outright.

The Crazy Place

Four tunnels lead here, one per element. Place the gramophone on the table at the end of a tunnel while holding that element's record, then run into the gateway — it falls apart after about 15 seconds.

You only need the gramophone once per element A gem can only be taken through its own element's tunnel, hence four trips. But once a player has used a portal, they can rebuild it from inside the Crazy Place without the gramophone.

The four staffs

Three parts plus an elemental gem, built on the lowest level of the Excavation Site. Then four upgrade sub-steps that are identical in shape for every staff.

Kagutsuchi's Blood P1 · red pedestal · charge shot leaves burning ground

Three parts

  • Generator 6 Rituals box — opens with the part the moment Gen 6 is powered.
  • The orange plane — spawns the round after the church path is opened. Shoot it down; the part lands beside the soul chest nearest the Excavation Site. It does not appear on rainy rounds, so if the sky is empty, wait one out.
  • The round-8 Panzersoldat — the only source, and it needs the No Man's Land door open beforehand.
Two different planes This orange one is visible to everyone. The step-5 plane only exists under Zombie Blood.

Record and tunnel

Red record spawns near the church: on the boxes between the tank and the staircase up, on the benches opposite the basin, or on boxes by Generator 6.

Fire tunnel is left of the spawn exit next to the Rituals box, on the way to Generator 3.

A

Crazy Place — burn 30 on the grates

Kill roughly 30 zombies with the Fire Staff standing on the metal grates in the fire section. Bodies burst into black smoke and fill the four cauldrons. This is the only riddle that is not a puzzle, and it consumes no souls, so every other staff should be sitting on its pedestal charging while you farm.

B

Church — the torch puzzle

The basement has seven torches, each with a number chalked under it. The 4 is a bloodstain rather than chalk, which catches everyone. Upstairs on the far wall, four symbols are lit; those four decode to the four torch numbers.

The numbers are not 1 to 7 A lot of guides say the torches run 1–7. They do not. They carry specific values — 3 4 5 6 7 9 11 — scattered along the basement walls. Read the number under the torch, do not count torches left to right.

Decoding, ternary: each symbol is columns of circles. Filled is 1, an outline is 0, two filled in one column is 2. Rightmost column is the 1s, next is the 3s, next is the 9s. So 1|1 is 4, 2|0 is 6, 1|0|0 is 9 and 1|0|2 is 11. 4 is always one of the four. Charts in Puzzle Keys.

Light all four with the staff. They must burn at the same time and the flames are short. Order does not matter, so relight whichever dies.

While you are down there Turn Lightning panel 2 (by torch 3, point it right) and upstairs panel 3 (by the repairable window, point it up) for P3.

Ull's Arrow P2 · blue pedestal · charged blizzard pulls and freezes

Three parts — dug, and only while it snows

One in the starting area (spawn through workshop, either side), one in the middle of the map, one near the church. Dig every pile in your zone while the snow lasts; results are random.

You set the squad's tempo Call it on comms every single time: snowing, nobody end the round. Missing the rounds 2 to 4 window can cost you six rounds of waiting.

Bad digs happen — a zombie, one live grenade, two live grenades. Step back the moment you press dig.

Record and tunnel

Blue record is at the Tank Station by Generator 2, on a shelf.

Ice tunnel is across from Generator 6, on the left as you exit the church. The Maxis Drone Frame can spawn right there in front of the portal.

A

Crazy Place — flip six panels

Six floating stone panels sit above the portal, each with a base-4 symbol. A blue ice panel on the wall shows a dot pattern in base 3. Read the pattern, find the floating panel with the matching value, shoot it with the Ice Staff. It flips and the wall shows the next one. Flip all six.

One miss resets everything Shoot the wrong panel and every flip so far reverts. Twelve patterns exist, six appear per game, and the pairing is randomised, so there is no memorisable answer. Have someone hold the horde while you read. Chart in Puzzle Keys.

  • Between the Excavation Site and a giant footprint, close to Gen 4.
  • On the small hill right of the Gen 2 bunker back exit, by the downed mech's hand.

Your superpower: the Zombie Blood farm

Shoot three flaming carts with the Ice Staff, upgraded or not, and a Zombie Blood spawns by the scaffolding near Pack-a-Punch.

  • Just outside the Excavation Site, by the MP-40 wall-buy.
  • By the footprint with the stone chest outside Gen 5 / Stamin-Up.
  • Outside the trenches behind the Excavation Site, more toward Gen 4.

Once per round. Never while it is raining. If nobody collects the power-up, the carts will not relight that round.

Why it matters Step 5 is entirely gated on this. And with a golden shovel under Zombie Blood you can see glowing orange dig spots that give Empty Perk Bottles — a fifth perk.
Kimat's Bite P3 · purple pedestal · chains between targets

The tank

Parked under the church near Gen 6. 500 points at the control panel on the tank itself; afterwards it can be called for 500 from the switch at either end. It needs about two minutes to cool down at each terminus, and fewer riders means a faster cooldown.

The tank ends rounds If the tank runs over the last zombie of a round, the round ends — and so does anyone else's window for a step. Do your runs while a teammate is holding a zombie well away from the muddy road.
Keep riding after you are done Once all three parts are collected, a random power-up spawns at each jump point once per round.

Three parts — one per robot

Freya (left, church), Odin (centre, Excavation), Thor (right, Gens 2 and 3). One part each.

  1. Watch a robot approach. One foot per pass glows, chosen at random.
  2. Shoot the glowing foot.
  3. Stand under it and let it step on you. You wake up in the head.
A foot you have not shot downs you instantly Ask the Ice player for the Golden Helmet as soon as they have a golden shovel. It makes robot feet harmless and turns this job, plus steps 2 and 3, into a formality.

Where the smoking balls are

A

Crazy Place — align four concentric rings

Four concentric rings sit above the portal, each carrying four symbols, and each pillar shows a target. Shoot a ring with the Wind Staff to rotate it until every row of symbols adds up to the value on the pillar it points at.

Reading base 4: count the lines in each shape. One shape is its own count. Two shapes are (first × 4) + last. Three shapes are (first × 16) + (second × 4) + last.

Shortcut The correct final arrangement never changes — only the starting rotation is random. Match the chart in Puzzle Keys instead of doing the arithmetic. Best vantage point is between the rocks to the right of the gateway.
B

Three smoking stone balls

Shoot each with the Wind Staff to rotate it until its smoke blows toward the Excavation Site.

  • Behind the Stamin-Up machine at Generator 5.
  • Between Generator 4 and the Wind tunnel.
  • On the outside section of No Man's Land, on the tank's return path.
Ball 3 has a side Approach it from the right direction or you push the smoke the wrong way. Backtrack the tank path from the church, or jump off the tank on its return. Coordinate with P3's run.

The eight steps

Steps 2 through 8 are strictly ordered. Step 7 pops the trophy and step 8 ends the game.

01

Secure the keys

Prerequisite phase · rounds 1–15

Build and upgrade all four staffs. Each is three parts plus an elemental gem, built at its coloured pedestal on the lowest level of the Excavation Site — which only opens once the gramophone plays the black record in the main chamber.

Silent failure The step only registers once all six generators have been live at the same time, at least once, after the last staff was built. If nothing ticks, re-cap everything.
02

Ascend from darkness

All four ultimate staffs required

Four pedestals appear and each accepts exactly one staff.

StaffGoes intoWhich robot
Ull's ArrowFreyaLeft, treads around the church
Boreas' FuryOdinCentre, treads the Excavation Site
Kimat's BiteThorRight, treads over Gens 2 and 3
Kagutsuchi's BloodStaff roomFourth pedestal, lowest Excavation level

Order does not matter. All six generators must be active or the pedestals will not despawn and the step will not register.

BO2-only shortcut Put the Fire Staff in the new staff-room pedestal, then press action on its original pedestal to take it straight back. Repeat four times and the game counts all four placements. Patched in BO3. Side effects are mostly visual.
03

Rain fire YOUR CALL

Two players · tight timing · needs a G-Strike
  1. Player A boards a robot and presses the red button inside its head. The Wind player is the natural pick.
  2. Player B, already at Generator 5 with a G-Strike primed, throws the beacon onto the seal — a circle of cracked stone on the ground behind and to the right of Gen 5, out of bounds.
  3. The robots shell the seal and crack it open.
If it misses A beacon thrown off-target vanishes and the Demonic Announcer laughs. After step 2 all three robots roam at once with only one lit foot between them, so expect several attempts.

Solo or duo: enter Odin from a footprint (the Stamin-Up side aims better), press the button as Maxis says one during the purge count — being purged is faster than ejecting — then sprint to the seal, priming on the way.

04

Unleash the horde

Boss wave · bring your best guns

Deploy the Maxis Drone as close to the broken seal as possible. He flies into the pit and ten Panzersoldats come out — that is the four-player count; solo gets two. Kill every one.

  • Upgraded staffs, Ray Gun Mark II, G-Strikes and Pack-a-Punched LMGs all pull their weight.
  • Split them up. Do not let two lock onto the same player.
  • Shoot the small red light on the claw arm to free a grabbed teammate. Meleeing the light works too.

The drone will not return to its station until you recover it in step 5.

05

Skewer the winged beast YOU SUPPLY IT

Gated entirely on Zombie Blood
  1. Get a Zombie Blood. The Ice player's flaming-cart farm is the repeatable source — once per round, never while raining.
  2. While it is active a glowing plane is visible above the Excavation Site. Shoot it down.
  3. The zombie pilot drops out and runs clockwise around the Excavation Site, visible only under Zombie Blood. Run counter-clockwise to meet him.
  4. Kill him. He drops the upgraded Maxis Drone with a Pack-a-Punched MG08/15.
Stack first Bank two or three Zombie Bloods before you start. The timer is short and you will probably need a second run.
06

Wield a fist of iron

Every player must complete this individually
  1. Everyone needs the One Inch Punch first — four soul chests at 30 kills each, claimed from a Rituals of the Ancients board.
  2. Go inside the Excavation Site. Crusaders now spawn with white glowing arms.
  3. Hit roughly 20 of them. The shockwave counts, so you do not have to kill them.
  4. A glowing white tablet drops. Pick it up for Iron Fists.
Run-ending mistake Do not punch Crusaders still climbing in through the walls. The tablet can drop out of reach and that makes the rest of the Easter Egg impossible. Pull them into the open first.

In BO2 the Iron Fists inherit the element of whatever staff you held when you picked up the tablet. Removed in BO3.

07

Raise hell

The trophy unlocks here

Place all four ultimate staffs on their pedestals inside the Crazy Place, then kill 100 Templar zombies in there. Souls stream into the centre, the screen flashes white, and the ceiling turns into a vortex.

Best hold-out in the map Once this is done the Crazy Place is wide open with no Panzers and no robots, and the achievement is already banked. Farm high rounds here if you want, because step 8 is a one-way door.
08

Freedom

Ends the match
  1. Deploy the Maxis Drone in the centre of the Crazy Place. He rises into the portal.
  2. Hold action on the blue rock in the centre to access the teleporter.
Point of no return This ends the game. Finish your camos, trophies and high rounds first. And if the six generators were not all live together at some point after step 5, the teleporter will not fire until you re-cap them.

Gear and buildables

Everything that is not a staff but still gates a step.

G-Strike beacon

Required for step 3
  1. Tank Station: take a dirty stone tablet. No prompt appears, just press action on it. There are four.
  2. Carry it to the church basin of holy water and insert it.
  3. 20 melee kills near the basin, then take the cleansed tablet.
  4. Return it to the Tank Station table without stepping in mud. Muddy means going back to rinse it.
  5. 20 more melee kills near the table and the beacon appears.
Do it early 40 melee kills at round 20 and up is misery. Get the One Inch Punch first if you can.

One Inch Punch

Every player needs it · gates step 6

Fill four soul chests, 30 kills each. Any player's kills count and a chest vanishes when full. Claim it at a Rituals of the Ancients board at Gen 1 or Gen 6: aim at the bottom-most medal and press action.

Shovels and the Golden Shovel YOUR JOB

Four shovels. Two always in spawn — by the starting radio, and in the corridor between a barrier and the Gen 3 door. The other two spawn at any two of: the church by the tank treads, the Wind tunnel by Gen 4, the Lightning tunnel by Gen 5.

Dig 30 spots in a game and your shovel turns gold. Better digs, fewer grenades, and two things that matter:

  • Golden Helmet — robot feet stop downing you. Hand it to the Wind player.
  • Empty Perk Bottles — glowing orange dig spots seen under Zombie Blood. A fifth perk.

House rules

Read this before you load in. Most failed runs die on one of these.

  • Nobody ends a round alone. Call it, agree, then end it.
  • Snow is sacred. While it snows the round stays open until the Ice player says otherwise.
  • Do not pick up Nukes. A Nuke wipes the map and can end a round mid-step. Same caution with Insta-Kill while someone is farming melee or chest kills.
  • Melee is required, not forbidden. The G-Strike needs 40 melee kills and step 6 needs about 20 punches.
  • Guns: keep the Mauser C96 and Pack-a-Punch it. Buy the MP40 early, at the Tank Station and near Gen 4. Both fall off hard past round 20.
  • Panzersoldat first spawns round 8 with the No Man's Land door open, then every 3 to 5 rounds. Immune to Monkey Bombs and G-Strikes.
  • Robot feet down you instantly unless you are wearing the Golden Helmet.
  • Staffs never break. Lose one and it returns to its pedestal, upgrade intact.
  • Step 7 gives the trophy, step 8 ends the game. Do everything else first.
