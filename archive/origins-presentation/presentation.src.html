Little LostGirl

8 Steps 4 Staffs 6 Generators Northern France · 1918

Free Samantha from Agartha. Four players, four elemental staffs, one dig site that gets exponentially harder every round.

This dossier is built for a squad of four running in parallel. Every job below is assigned to someone, and almost nothing has to wait its turn. Pick your staff and the page marks your jobs everywhere they appear.

Written for Black Ops II (PS5 / backwards-compatible release). Everything applies to the Black Ops III Zombies Chronicles remaster too — the handful of differences are listed under Rules.

The two hard clocks Snow gates every Ice Staff part — it snows around rounds 2–4, again near round 10, then at random. Round 8 is the only Panzersoldat that drops a Fire Staff part, and it only spawns if the No Man's Land door is already open. Miss either and you burn rounds.

02

The Eight Steps

Steps 2 through 8 are strictly ordered. Step 7 pops the trophy; step 8 ends the game.

01
Prerequisite phase  ·  rounds 1–15

Secure the keys

Build and upgrade all four elemental staffs. Each staff is three parts plus an elemental gem from the Crazy Place, built at its coloured pedestal on the lowest level of the Excavation Site — which only opens once the gramophone plays the black record in the main chamber.

Silent failure The step only registers once all six generators have been live at the same time, at least once, after the last staff was built. If nothing ticks, re-cap everything.

Build these in parallel while the staffs come together: G-Strike beacon (needed for step 3), One Inch Punch (every player needs it for step 6), Maxis Drone (steps 4 and 8).

02
All four ultimate staffs required

Four pedestals appear. Each accepts exactly one staff.

To board a robot: shoot the glowing foot, then let that robot step on you. One foot per pass is lit, chosen at random. Order of placement doesn't matter.

BO2-only shortcut Put the Fire Staff in the new staff-room pedestal, then press action on its original pedestal to take it straight back. Repeat four times — the game counts all four placements. Patched in BO3. Mostly cosmetic side effects.
03
Two players  ·  tight timing

Rain fire

  1. Player A boards a robot and presses the red button inside its head. Wind player is the natural pick
  2. Player B — already standing at Generator 5, G-Strike primed — throws the beacon onto the seal: a circle of cracked stone on the ground behind and to the right of Gen 5, out of bounds.
  3. The robots shell the seal and crack it open.
If it misses A beacon thrown off-target vanishes and the Demonic Announcer laughs. Reset and retry. After step 2 all three robots roam at once with only one lit foot between them — expect several attempts.

Solo or duo: enter Odin from a footprint (the Stamin-Up side aims better), press the button as Maxis says one during the purge count — being purged is faster than ejecting — then sprint to the seal priming on the way.

04
Boss wave  ·  bring your best guns

Unleash the horde

Deploy the Maxis Drone as close to the broken seal as you can. He flies into the pit and ten Panzersoldats come out (four-player count; solo gets two). Kill every one.

  • Upgraded staffs, Ray Gun Mark II, G-Strikes and Pack-a-Punched LMGs all pull their weight here.
  • Split them up — do not let two lock onto the same player.
  • Shoot the small red light on the claw arm to free a grabbed teammate. Meleeing the light works too.

The drone will not return to its station until you recover it in step 5.

05
Needs Zombie Blood Ice player supplies it

Skewer the winged beast

  1. Get a Zombie Blood. Repeatable source: the Ice player shoots the three flaming carts — once per round, and never while it is raining.
  2. While it is active a glowing plane is visible above the Excavation Site. Shoot it down.
  3. The zombie pilot drops out and runs clockwise around the Excavation Site. He is only visible in Zombie Blood. Run counter-clockwise to meet him.
  4. Kill him — he drops the upgraded Maxis Drone with a Pack-a-Punched MG08/15.
Stack firstBank two or three Zombie Bloods before you start. The timer is short and you will likely need a second run.
06
Every player must complete this

Wield a fist of iron

  1. Everyone needs the One Inch Punch first — four soul chests at 30 kills each, then claim it from a Rituals of the Ancients board.
  2. Go inside the Excavation Site. Crusaders now spawn with white glowing arms.
  3. Hit roughly 20 of them. The shockwave counts — you don't have to kill them.
  4. A glowing white tablet drops. Pick it up → Iron Fists.
Run-ending mistake Do not punch Crusaders that are still climbing in through the walls. The tablet can drop out of reach, and that makes the rest of the easter egg impossible. Pull them into the open first.

In BO2 the Iron Fists inherit the element of whatever staff you held when you picked up the tablet — hold the Fire Staff and your punches set zombies alight. Removed in BO3.

07
The trophy unlocks here

Raise hell

Place all four ultimate staffs on their pedestals inside the Crazy Place, then kill 100 Templar zombies in there. Their souls stream into the centre; the screen flashes white and the ceiling turns into a vortex.

Best hold-out in the map Once this is done the Crazy Place is wide open, with no Panzers and no robots — and the achievement is already banked. Farm high rounds here if you want to, because step 8 is a one-way door.
08
Ends the match

Freedom

  1. Deploy the Maxis Drone in the centre of the Crazy Place. He rises into the portal.
  2. Hold action on the blue rock in the centre to access the teleporter.
Point of no return This ends the game. Finish your camos, trophies and high rounds first. And if the six generators weren't all live together at some point after step 5, the teleporter won't fire until you re-cap them.

You get a different ending track, a camera pan into the portal, and a special cutscene after the scoreboard.

A

Crazy Place — burn 30 on the grates

Kill roughly 30 zombies with the Fire Staff on the metal grates in the fire section. Corpses burst into black smoke and fill the four cauldrons. No puzzle — and no souls consumed, so every other staff should be sitting on its pedestal charging while you do it.

B

Church — the torch puzzle

The basement has seven torches numbered 1–7. The 4 is a bloodstain, not chalk — that catches everyone. Upstairs on the far wall, four symbols are lit; those four are your torch numbers.

Decoding (ternary): each symbol is columns of circles. Filled = 1, outline = 0, two filled in one column = 2. Left column is the 3s place, right is the 1s place. So 1|1 = 4, 1|2 = 5, 2|0 = 6, 2|1 = 7. 4 is always one of them.

Light all four torches with the staff. They must burn at the same time and the flames are short — order doesn't matter, relight whichever dies.

While you're down thereTurn Lightning panel #2 (by torch 3, point it right) and upstairs panel #3 (by the repairable window, point it up) for P3.
C

Four levers on the lower Excavation level — two on the walkway, two on the scaffolding. All four ring lights red, then shoot the red orb with the Fire Staff.

D

Charge — red pedestal, ~20 kills

Place it on the red pedestal in the Crazy Place and farm ~20 kills inside. Samantha calls it out, the HUD icon gets a red outline, and Kagutsuchi's Blood is yours.

Three parts — dug, and only while it snows

One in the starting area (spawn through workshop, either side), one in the middle of the map, one near the church. Dig every pile in your zone while the snow lasts — the results are random.

You set the tempoSnow lands around rounds 2–4, again near round 10, then at random. Call it on comms every single time: "snowing, nobody end the round."

A

Crazy Place — flip six panels

Six floating stone panels sit above the portal, each carrying a base-4 symbol. A blue ice panel on the wall shows a dot pattern (base 3). Read the pattern, find the floating panel with the matching value, shoot it with the Ice Staff. It flips; the wall shows the next one. Flip all six.

One miss resets everythingShoot the wrong panel and every flipped panel reverts. Twelve patterns exist, six appear per game, and the pairing is randomised — there is no memorisable answer. Have someone hold the horde while you read.
B

Three water gravestones

Freeze each with the Ice Staff, then destroy it with a bullet weapon. Don't run staff-only.

  • Right of the MP40 wall-buy near Generator 4.
  • Between the Excavation Site and a giant footprint, close to Gen 4.
  • On the small hill right of the Gen 2 bunker back exit, by the downed mech's hand.

Three parts — one in each robot's head

Freya (left, church), Odin (centre, Excavation), Thor (right, Gens 2 & 3). One part each.

  1. Watch a robot approach. One foot per pass glows, chosen at random.
  2. Shoot the glowing foot.
  3. Stand under it and let it step on you → you're in the head.
A foot you haven't shot downs you instantlyAsk the Ice player for the Golden Helmet as soon as they have a golden shovel — it makes robot feet harmless and turns this job, plus steps 2 and 3, into a formality.

While inside Freya there's a red radio opposite the audio log — one of the three for the Shepherd of Fire music easter egg.

A

Crazy Place — align four concentric rings

Four concentric rings above the portal, each carrying four symbols; each pillar shows a target. Shoot a ring with the Wind Staff to rotate it, until every row of symbols adds up to the value on the pillar it points at.

Reading base 4: count the lines in each shape. One shape = its count. Two shapes = (first × 4) + last. Three shapes = (first × 16) + (second × 4) + last.

ShortcutThe correct final arrangement never changes — only the starting rotation is random. Match the key image instead of doing the arithmetic. Best vantage point: between the rocks to the right of the gateway.
B

Three smoking stone balls

Ball 3 has a sideApproach it from the right direction or you'll push the smoke the wrong way — backtrack the tank path from the church, or jump off the tank on its return. Coordinate with P3's run.

Gear & Buildables

Everything that isn't a staff but still gates a step.

G-Strike Beacon YOUR JOB TOO

Required for step 3  ·  all four players can run one at once
  1. Tank Station: take a dirty stone tablet. No prompt appears — just press action on it. There are four.
  2. Carry it to the church basin of holy water and insert it.
  3. 20 melee kills near the basin, then take the cleansed tablet.
  4. Return it to the Tank Station table without stepping in mud. Muddy means going back to re-cleanse.
  5. 20 more melee kills near the table → beacon.
Do it early40 melee kills at round 20+ is miserable. Get the One Inch Punch first if you can. Riding the tank is the clean way across the mud; otherwise stick to the wooden beams.

Two beacons in BO2, three in BO3. Max Ammo refills them; the Mystery Box can give them back if you lose them.

One Inch Punch → Iron Fists

Every player needs it  ·  gates step 6

Fill four soul chests, 30 kills each. Any player's kills count, and a chest vanishes when full. All four sit in giant footprints:

  • Near Gen 5 / Stamin-Up.
  • On the Excavation → church path — the only one outside Odin's lane.
  • On the path from the No Man's Land entrance to Gen 4.
  • In the map corner by Gen 4 / MP-40.
Two ways to waste 30 killsA robot stepping on an unfilled chest closes it and resets progress. And zombies spawned from dig spots don't count at all.

Claim it at a Rituals of the Ancients board (Gen 1 or Gen 6): aim at the bottom-most medal and press action.

Shovels & the Golden Shovel YOUR JOB

Four shovels. Two always in spawn — by the starting radio, and in the corridor between a barrier and the Gen 3 door. The other two spawn at any two of: the church by the tank treads · the Wind tunnel by Gen 4 · the Lightning tunnel by Gen 5.

06

House Rules

Read this before you load in. Most failed runs die on one of these.

  • Nobody ends a round alone. Call it, agree, then end it.
  • Snow is sacred. If it's snowing, the round stays open until the Ice player says otherwise.
  • Don't pick up Nukes. A Nuke wipes the map and can end a round mid-step. Same caution with Insta-Kill while someone is farming melee or chest kills.
  • Melee is required, not forbidden. The G-Strike needs 40 melee kills and step 6 needs ~20 punches.
  • Guns: keep the Mauser C96 and Pack-a-Punch it — the upgrade is genuinely good. Buy the MP40 early (Tank Station, and near Gen 4). Both fall off hard past round 20; by then you want ultimate staffs, a Pack-a-Punched LMG or the Ray Gun Mark II.
  • Panzersoldat first spawns round 8 (only with the No Man's Land door open), then every 3–5 rounds. Shoot the red light on its claw to free a teammate. Immune to Monkey Bombs and G-Strikes.
  • Robot feet down you instantly unless you're wearing the Golden Helmet.
  • Staffs never break. Lose one and it returns to its pedestal, upgrade intact.
  • Step 7 gives the trophy. Step 8 ends the game. Do everything else first.
