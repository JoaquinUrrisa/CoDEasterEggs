🔴 Fire Staff Player Guide (P1)

A pruned copy of the full guide, focused only on what the Fire Staff player needs.

Your zone: Church / Generator 6 side. Your staff: Staff of Fire → Kagutsuchi's Blood. Your extra jobs: shoot down the orange plane, kill the round-8 Panzersoldat, grab the red record, open the church path and cap Generator 6.

Two of your three parts are time-gated (the plane spawns the round after the church path opens; the Panzer part only drops on round 8). You are on a clock — push the church early.

Build the Staff of Fire

Built at the red pedestal, lowest level of the Excavation Site (opened with the gramophone + black record).

Parts

1 — Generator 6 Rituals of the Ancients box. Power Generator 6 (behind the church, furthest from spawn). The reward box next to it opens with the part inside.

2 — The orange glowing plane. It spawns in the sky the round after the path to the church is opened. Shoot it down. The part drops next to the soul chest closest to the Excavation Site, on the small walkway between two giant footprints.

Any gun works. Look up over the Excavation Site; it's clearly glowing orange. Don't confuse it with the Step 5 plane, which is only visible in Zombie Blood.

3 — The round-8 Panzersoldat. The first Panzer always spawns on round 8 — but only if the door to No Man's Land is already open. If you open it after round 8, the Panzer comes the next round. Kill it and it drops the part. This is the only source.

Shoot the red light on its claw arm to free a grabbed teammate. Melee-range and it will flamethrower you. Juggernog strongly recommended before round 8.

Red record

Near the church, one of:

  • On the boxes between the tank and the staircase up (hard to spot if the tank is parked there)
  • On the church benches opposite the basin
  • On the boxes next to Generator 6

Fire tunnel → red gem

The Fire tunnel is left of the spawn exit, next to the Rituals of the Ancients box, on the way to Generator 3. The gramophone carrier (P4) places the gramophone on the table at the end of the tunnel with the red record → portal builds itself → run through it.

Inside the Crazy Place the red pedestal opens and the fire gem rises out. Take it. The gem is only obtainable if you entered through the Fire tunnel.

Then build the staff at the red pedestal on the lowest level of the Excavation Site.

Upgrade to Kagutsuchi's Blood

A — Crazy Place riddle (the easy one)

Kill ~30 zombies with the Fire Staff on the metal grates in the fire section of the Crazy Place. Done correctly, corpses burst into black smoke that fills the four cauldrons nearby.

This step doesn't consume souls.Tell your teammates to leave their staffs on their pedestals while you farm here — they charge (step D) off the same kills. Best coordinated moment of the game.

B — Church torches puzzle

The church basement has seven torches, each with a number chalked under it. The "4" is a bloodstain, not chalk — that trips people up.

The numbers are not 1–7.Plenty of guides say they are. They aren't. The torches carry specific values — 3 4 5 6 7 9 11 — scattered along the basement walls. Read the number under the torch; don't count torches left to right.

Upstairs, on the far wall, there is a set of symbols; four of them are lit. Those four symbols are the four torch numbers you must light.

Decoding (ternary / base 3):

  • Each symbol is columns of circles. Filled circle = 1, outline = 0, two filled circles in a column = 2.
  • Rightmost column = the 1s, next left = the 3s, next = the 9s.
  • So: 1|1 = 4, 2|0 = 6, 1|0|0 = 9, 1|0|2 = 11.
  • 4 is always one of the four numbers.

Lighting them: shoot each of the four torches with the Fire Staff. All four must be lit at the same time and the flames burn out fast. Order doesn't matter — if one dies, relight it. When it works you get an audio cue and a beam of light shoots up from the Excavation Site.

Read the upstairs symbols first, write the four numbers down, then go to the basement. 💡 While you're in the basement, turn Lightning panel #2 (next to torch 3, point it right) and upstairs panel #3 (next to the repairable window, point it up) for P3.

C — Ring puzzle (Excavation Site, lower level)

Four floating rings. Four levers rotate them — two on the normal walkway, two on the wooden scaffolding. Turn them until all four ring lights are red, then shoot the red orb inside the rings with the Fire Staff. It glows red and shoots into the sky.

Only one element's orb can be active at a time. Queue with your teammates — one staff at a time.

D — Charge in the Crazy Place

Place the staff on the red pedestal in the Crazy Place and kill ~20 zombies inside. Samantha announces "the power of fire", the HUD icon gets a red outline, and you can pick up Kagutsuchi's Blood.

Your weapon

Fires three horizontal molten rocks. Upgraded: hold fire to charge for a bigger blast that leaves burning ground before a second explosion; point-blank vaporises; melee is a one-hit kill until round 16. Press left on the D-pad for Sekhmet's Vigor — flip the staff and shoot a downed teammate to revive them at range.

With PhD Flopper (Der Wunderfizz, BO2 only), dive to prone for a fire explosion.

If you lose the staff, it returns to its Excavation Site pedestal with the upgrade intact.

Shared jobs (everyone does these)

G-Strike beacon — do it early

  1. Tank Station (Gen 2) — take a dirty stone tablet (no prompt, just press action). There are four, one per player.
  2. Carry it to the church basin of holy water, insert it.
  3. 20 melee kills near the basin → take the cleansed tablet back.
  4. Return it to the Tank Station table without stepping in mud (stick to the wooden beams). Muddy = go re-cleanse it at the basin.
  5. 20 more melee kills near the table → G-Strike Beacon.

You're the church player, so you're already sitting on step 2/3 — run this while doing the torches.

Soul chests → One Inch Punch

Four chests, 30 kills each, in giant footprints: Gen 5 / Stamin-Up · Excavation→church path (the safe one, not in Odin's lane) · No Man's Land entrance→Gen 4 · map corner by Gen 4 / MP-40. Claim the Punch at a Rituals of the Ancients board (Gen 1 or Gen 6) — aim at the bottom-most medal.

A robot stepping on an unfinished chest resets it. Dig-spot zombies don't count.

Easter egg steps — your part

  • Step 2: the Fire staff goes in the staff room pedestal (the fourth one, in front of the original four on the lowest Excavation level) — not in a robot.
  • Step 3: you can be either the button-presser (inside a robot) or the G-Strike thrower at the seal behind/right of Gen 5. Coordinate.
  • Step 6: you need your own Iron Fists — ~20 One Inch Punch hits on white-glowing Crusaders inside the Excavation Site. Don't punch ones still climbing through walls.
  • Step 7: put Kagutsuchi's Blood on its Crazy Place pedestal, help kill 100 Templars inside.

Reminders

  • Don't end a round while it's snowing (ice player's parts).
  • Don't grab Nukes.
  • Keep the Mauser C96 and Pack-a-Punch it; buy the MP40 early.
  • Juggernog before round 8. The Panzer is your part and it hits hard.
